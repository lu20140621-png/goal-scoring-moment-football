1. Goal & Winning1. 游戏目标与获胜

There is only one FOOTBALL Card. When you draw it, announce it aloud and allow your opponents time to react. If the score is not stopped, take one of the 8 mini footballs from the center. After the draw effect is fully resolved, shuffle the FOOTBALL Card back into the draw pile.

实体FOOTBALL卡只有一张。抽到后必须喊出来，给对手响应时间；若无人成功阻止，则从桌面中央共8个迷你橄榄球中拿1个。当前抽牌效果全部结算后，把FOOTBALL卡洗回抽牌堆。

  1. When all 8 mini footballs have been claimed from the center, end regular play and compare each player's footballs.
  2. Or, when the 15-minute timer expires, end regular play and compare footballs.
  3. Whichever occurs first ends regular play. The player holding the most mini footballs wins.
  1. 公共区8个迷你橄榄球全部拿完，立即结束常规赛，比较各玩家持有数量。
  2. 或者15分钟时间到，立即结束常规赛，比较数量。
  3. 以先触发的结束条件为准。持有最多者获胜。
Tie for first: Only players tied for the lead enter a 10-minute Football Scramble tiebreaker. Divide the Magic Cards evenly among those players. No buying Magic Cards with mini footballs during the tiebreaker. The previously defined detailed Football Scramble steps still need to be inserted from the earlier rules.
并列第一：只有并列第一的玩家进入10分钟抢球大战，魔法卡平均分发给参赛者，抢球大战期间不能用迷你橄榄球换魔法卡。之前制定的抢球大战具体步骤还需补入（当前未找到可核对版本）。

2. Setup — Follow This Order2. 开局准备——严格按顺序

  1. Set the Animal Role Cards aside; roles are not used in Mode 3. One unused Role Card may later serve as a Phantom Pass trap.
  2. Shuffle Magic Cards separately. Deal one random Magic Card to each player. Put the unused Magic Cards in their own face-down supply.
  3. Shuffle all Action Cards and numbered Scoring Cards together.
  4. From that mixed deck, deal five cards to each player.
  5. After dealing all five-card hands, insert the single FOOTBALL Card into the remaining regular draw pile and reshuffle.
  6. Place 8 mini footballs in the center, choose the starting player and direction, and start the 15-minute timer as play begins.
  1. 身份卡不用，先放一旁；只有Phantom Pass可能使用其中1张作陷阱。
  2. 魔法卡单独洗牌，随机每人发1张；剩余魔法卡单独放成备用堆。
  3. 把所有功能卡和数字得分卡混合洗牌。
  4. 从混合牌堆每人发5张。
  5. 发完所有人的5张手牌后，才将唯一一张FOOTBALL卡插入剩余公共抽牌堆，再重新洗牌。
  6. 桌面中央摆好8个迷你橄榄球，决定先手与方向，开局启动15分钟倒计时。
Keep these separate: the regular draw/discard piles; unused Magic Cards for exchange; and a face-up Magic discard pile where used spells can be seen and copied.
分开放：普通抽牌堆和弃牌堆；未使用的备用魔法堆；以及使用过的公开魔法弃牌区。

3. Every Turn — Step by Step3. 每个回合——一步一步玩

  1. During your own turn, you may play multiple Action Cards, numbered Scoring Cards and eligible Magic Cards. You can play more than one Magic Card if you own more than one.
  2. Resolve one card fully before playing the next. Run, Pass, Tackle, Strip and Spell Swap are own-turn only. Interception is a reaction when another player resolves Pass; Block reacts to Tackle or Interception. Other reaction Magic Cards have their own triggers.
  3. At the end of your turn, draw one card. This is the normal end-of-turn draw. A card that explicitly ends your turn (such as the 10-point card) overrides this sequence.
  4. Whenever FOOTBALL is drawn, call it out, give opponents time to react, and only then resolve the mini-football award. When that entire draw effect is finished, put FOOTBALL back in the pile and reshuffle.
  5. If the regular draw pile runs out, shuffle the regular discard pile into a new draw pile. During any multi-card draw, keep already-drawn cards outside the pile until all cards in that draw have been taken. One physical FOOTBALL cannot appear multiple times among the same five simultaneously drawn cards.
  1. 自己回合可打出多张功能卡、多张数字得分卡及多张符合时机的魔法卡；拥有多张魔法卡时可以使用多张。
  2. 每次只结算一张牌，结算完再出下一张。Run、Pass、Tackle、Strip、Spell Swap只能自己回合使用。Interception只能在别人Pass时响应；Block用于抵消Tackle或Interception。其他响应魔法按技能时机发动。
  3. 正常回合结束时抽1张卡。若打出了明确“立即结束回合”的10分卡，按该卡执行。
  4. 抽到FOOTBALL要喊出来并留给对手响应时间，结算后才能领球。当前整个抽牌效果完成后，再把FOOTBALL洗回公共牌堆。
  5. 抽牌堆没牌时，将普通弃牌堆洗好重新组成抽牌堆。一次要抽多张（比如30分抽5张）时，已抽出来的牌先离开牌堆，等整次抽完才洗回其中的FOOTBALL，同一张实体FOOTBALL不可能同时出现在这5张里多次。
Buying Magic: Every player starts with one randomly dealt spell. An additional unused Magic Card costs one mini football. Spells are one-use only; after use, place them face up in the Magic discard pile. Copycat may copy an effect from that pile, without reclaiming the physical card. No Magic exchange is allowed in the tiebreaker.
魔法换购：每人开局随机有1张。之后1个迷你橄榄球换1张未使用魔法卡。魔法卡一张只用一次，用后正面朝上放在魔法弃牌区；Copycat只复制该区域的效果，不能拿回实体卡。抢球大战禁止换购。
Details not yet specified: Exactly when Magic can be purchased and where paid mini footballs go. Those details affect how the eight-football supply empties.
待确认：何时可以买魔法、花掉的迷你橄榄球放回公共区还是移出游戏？这直接影响“8个球被拿完”的结算。
Scoring Card numbers (30 / 20 / 10 / 6 / 3 / 1) are card labels, not victory points.
得分卡数字30／20／10／6／3／1只用于区分技能，不计入胜负分数。

3A. Complete Playtest Flow · Proposed3A. 完整试玩流程 · 建议版

Suggested temporary defaults, not yet designer-approved. Use these steps to test a full game while the final starting-hand and timing details are being decided. The card effects shown below remain unchanged.

以下是临时试玩建议，尚未获得你的最终确认。 先用这套流程完整测试一局，之后再确定正式手牌和时序。下方卡牌技能不变。

  1. Deal: Shuffle Action Cards, Scoring Cards and the single FOOTBALL Card together. For this test, deal five regular cards to each player. If FOOTBALL appears during setup, return it to the deck, reshuffle and replace it without scoring. Separately deal one Magic Card to each player.
  2. Start: Put eight mini footballs in the center and start a 15-minute timer. Choose a first player; start clockwise.
  3. Your turn: For this test, play at most one Action or numbered Scoring Card from your hand, or skip playing a card. Your one-use Magic Card may be played at its stated timing. Reaction cards may be used during other players' turns when triggered.
  4. Finish: After resolving the card, draw one card to end your turn. Exception: the 10-point card ends your turn immediately and grants three draws at the start of your next turn. Resolve bonus draws one at a time.
  5. FOOTBALL: Pause before awarding the mini football so eligible reactions can be played. During PASS, first decide who receives each card. If the FOOTBALL draw survives reactions, its recipient takes one available mini football. Do not hold FOOTBALL in your hand after resolution.
  6. Recycle: For this safer playtest, put FOOTBALL into the discard pile after its draw resolves; Goalpost Warp instead puts it directly below the top card of the draw pile. When ten or fewer cards remain in the draw pile, shuffle the discard pile into it. Used Action/Scoring Cards are discarded as usual. Spent Magic Cards stay out of play.
  7. End: Stop immediately after the last of the eight mini footballs is claimed, or at 15 minutes (finish the effect already in progress for this test). The most mini footballs wins. For this test only, a tie is a shared win until a tiebreaker is agreed.
  1. 发牌：功能卡、得分卡和唯一的FOOTBALL一起洗牌。试玩建议每人发5张普通手牌；如果发牌时出现FOOTBALL，放回重洗并补发，不计分。魔法卡另外每人发1张。
  2. 开局：中央放8个迷你橄榄球，开启15分钟计时；决定先手，默认顺时针。
  3. 自己的回合：试玩建议主动使用最多1张功能卡或数字得分卡，也可以不出。魔法卡按其时机使用一次；反应牌允许在别人回合触发时使用。
  4. 结束回合：正常抽1张结束回合；10分卡例外，立即结束本回合，下次自己回合开始先抽3张。额外抽牌逐张结算。
  5. 抽到FOOTBALL：先暂停，让其他玩家响应。PASS先决定两张牌分别归谁；若FOOTBALL未被阻止，最终获得该卡的玩家从公共区拿1个迷你橄榄球。FOOTBALL结算后不留在手里。
  6. 牌堆回收：为避免FOOTBALL在小牌堆里连续重复得分，试玩时建议FOOTBALL结算后放入弃牌堆；Goalpost Warp例外，直接插到抽牌堆顶下方1张。抽牌堆剩下10张或更少时，将弃牌堆洗回。其他用过的功能卡、得分卡照常弃置；用过的魔法卡退出本局。
  7. 结束：8个球领完立即结束，或15分钟到时完成正在结算的一次能力后比较球数。试玩版并列第一可暂时共同获胜，平局规则等待定稿。
Not final: Initial hand size, number of main actions per turn, reaction priority, duration of “this round,” 20-point cancellation scope, and the PASS/FOOTBALL response sequence require designer approval.
仍待你确认：开局手牌数、每回合主动出牌上限、反应顺序、“本轮”的范围、20分卡能取消哪些能力、PASS遇到FOOTBALL的精确时序。

4. All 6 Action Cards4. 六种功能卡

Run

Draw 1 card.

抽1张卡。

Pass

Draw 2 cards and look at both. Choose 1 to give to a player of your choice; keep the other.

抽2张卡，自己查看后，选择其中1张交给指定玩家，另1张自己留下。

Tackle

Take 1 card from any player's hand. You may also play Tackle as a reaction to steal the card another player just drew with Run.

从任意玩家手中抽走1张卡。也可以在别人使用Run时，抢走对方通过Run抽到的那张卡。

Interception

When another player uses Pass, take the card that player chose to keep.

当其他玩家使用Pass时，拿走他选择留给自己的那张卡。

Block

When you are targeted by Tackle or Interception, discard Block to cancel that effect.

受到Tackle或Interception影响时，可以弃置Block，取消这次效果。

Strip

Reverse the direction of play.

立即反转游戏方向：顺时针改逆时针，逆时针改顺时针。实体卡名称固定为Strip。

5. All 6 Scoring Card Abilities5. 六种得分卡能力

30-POINT CARD

Play this card to draw 5 cards immediately.

使用后立即抽5张卡。

20-POINT CARD

Cancel the ability of any one card.

禁止任意一张卡牌的能力。

10-POINT CARD

End your turn immediately. On your next turn, draw 3 cards.

立即结束自己的回合；下次轮到自己时抽3张卡。

6-POINT CARD

Privately look at the top 5 cards of the shared deck. Rearrange those 5 cards in any order and put them back on top.

自己秘密查看牌堆顶部5张卡，可以自由改变这5张卡的顺序，然后放回牌顶。

3-POINT CARD

Reveal the top 3 cards to everyone. Keep their order unchanged and return them to the top of the deck.

公开查看牌堆顶部3张卡，让所有玩家看到；顺序不变，放回牌顶。

1-POINT CARD

Reshuffle the shared draw pile, then draw 1 card.

重新洗混公共抽牌堆，然后抽1张卡。

6. All 20 Magic Cards6. 二十种魔法卡

Shuffle Magic Cards separately and deal one to each player at setup. Each spell is one-use only; after it resolves, remove that Magic Card from the game. These are Mode 3 effects, not Game Day or Quick Play effects.

开局魔法卡单独洗牌，每人1张。整局仅可使用一次，用后移出游戏。以下是模式3的独立效果，不套用模式1、2的效果。

01 · Time Stop

Play on your turn. Take an extra turn immediately after this one.

自己回合使用，额外获得1回合。

02 · Goalpost Warp

When another player reveals FOOTBALL, call “Goalpost Warp!” Put that card back into the draw pile one card below the top. This draw does not award a mini football.

别人抽到FOOTBALL准备结算时喊“球门移动”，将卡放回牌堆牌顶下方一张，本次不获得迷你橄榄球。

03 · Flight

Look through the draw pile to locate the FOOTBALL Card. Restore the exact original order.

查看牌堆，找出橄榄球卡在哪里；只能看，不可改变顺序。

04 · Gravity

For this round, everyone except you must use twice as many cards to activate a normal card ability.

本轮除自己外，其他玩家要使用双倍卡牌，才能发动普通卡牌能力。

05 · Magic Barrier

For this round, other effects cannot cancel your cards or take cards from your hand.

本轮其他人无法禁止你的卡牌能力，也不能抽走你的手牌。

06 · Shatter

When another player draws FOOTBALL, call “Shatter!” That player does not receive a mini football from this draw.

有人抽到FOOTBALL时喊“魔法粉碎”，这次抽牌不会获得迷你橄榄球。

07 · Time Rewind

Play on your turn. Take an extra turn immediately after this one.

自己回合使用，额外获得1回合。

08 · Invincibility

For this round, other effects cannot cancel your cards or take cards from your hand.

本轮其他人无法禁止你的卡牌能力，也不能抽走你的手牌。

09 · Phantom Pass

Insert 1 unused Animal Role Card into the draw pile as a trap. If someone draws it, take 1 mini football from that player; if nobody draws it, the trap expires unused.

拿1张闲置身份卡插入牌堆作为陷阱。有人抽到时，拿走该玩家1个迷你橄榄球；若本局没人抽到，陷阱失效。

10 · True Sight

Look through the draw pile to locate the FOOTBALL Card. Restore the exact original order.

查看牌堆，找出橄榄球卡在哪里；只能看，不可改变顺序。

11 · Gale Force

For this round, everyone except you must use twice as many cards to activate a normal card ability.

本轮除自己外，其他玩家要使用双倍卡牌，才能发动普通卡牌能力。

12 · Titan's Might

For your current turn, double the number of cards drawn by Action Card abilities. Scoring Card draws are not doubled.

本回合功能卡能力产生的抽牌数量×2；得分卡产生的抽牌不翻倍。

13 · Chaos

All players shuffle their hands into the shared draw pile, then each draws the same number of cards they returned. Anyone who draws FOOTBALL gains a mini football.

所有人把自己的手牌洗回公共抽牌堆，再各自抽取相同数量的卡。任何人抽到FOOTBALL，都获得1个迷你橄榄球。

14 · Arcane Heist

Steal 1 card from the hand of each other player.

从在场每一名其他玩家手中各窃取1张卡。

15 · Guardian

For this round, other effects cannot cancel your cards or take cards from your hand.

本轮其他人无法禁止你的卡牌能力，也不能抽走你的手牌。

16 · Copycat

Copy the effect of a Magic Card already used in this game. Resolve that effect immediately; you cannot save it for later.

复制本局已经使用过的一张魔法卡能力，并立即使用，不能保留到以后。

17 · Spell Swap

When someone else draws FOOTBALL, play this spell and exchange one card from your hand for that FOOTBALL Card.

别人抽到FOOTBALL时发动，用自己手里1张卡交换他的橄榄球卡。

18 · Invisibility

For this round, draw 1 card on each player's turn—even if that player draws nothing or plays no Action Card.

本轮每当任何玩家轮到回合，你都能从牌堆抽1张卡，无论该玩家是否抽牌或使用功能卡。

19 · Roll of Fate

Roll 2 dice. If their total is greater than 6, take 1 available mini football from the shared supply.

掷2个骰子，点数之和大于6，从公共区领取1个迷你橄榄球。

20 · Mystic Fog

Steal 1 mini football from a player of your choice.

从指定的一名玩家手中偷走1个迷你橄榄球。

Magic names remain unchanged. Repeated spell abilities are shown exactly as currently specified; they have not been silently redesigned.
魔法卡名称全部保留。能力相同的魔法也按你目前指定的内容保留，没有擅自改动。

7. Details to Confirm Before Finalizing7. 定稿前需要确认的细节

Critical card-loop warning: Do not return FOOTBALL immediately to a one-card draw pile between the five draws of a 30-point card. That would allow the same FOOTBALL Card to earn as many as five mini footballs in one action. Discard-and-recycle reduces the risk but does not fully prevent it when only FOOTBALL can be recycled. A separate safeguard is still needed: for example, at most one mini football awarded per card effect, or never reshuffle FOOTBALL alone.
重要漏洞提醒：30分卡连续抽5张时，不要将FOOTBALL立即洗回一个只剩FOOTBALL的牌堆，否则同一张卡可能在一次行动中连续领取5个迷你橄榄球。“得分后先弃置、10张以下再洗回”只能降低风险；如果最终仍只有FOOTBALL可洗回，漏洞还会存在。彻底解决还需另外确认：例如每次卡牌能力最多产生1个迷你橄榄球，或禁止只将FOOTBALL单独洗回。
  1. Starting hand and turn flow: Number of Action/Scoring Cards dealt, cards played per turn, mandatory draw and hand limit.
  2. FOOTBALL recycling: Only one physical FOOTBALL Card exists. The suggested playtest method discards it after scoring and recycles discards when ten or fewer cards remain in the draw pile. Confirm this as the final method, and clarify whether a replacement card is drawn after scoring.
  3. Reaction priority: If Goalpost Warp, Shatter, Spell Swap, Tackle or Interception affect the same draw, which resolves first? How does shifting a FOOTBALL Card back into the deck work during a two-card Pass draw?
  4. Round duration: Does “this round” last through the current lap of players or until the caster's next turn?
  5. Gravity/Gale Force: Must players discard two cards of the same type? Does this apply to Scoring Cards or one-use Magic Cards?
  6. Extra-turn timing: Do Time Stop and Time Rewind take effect immediately after the current turn?
  7. Discard / tie: What if the draw pile runs out? How is a tie for most footballs settled?
  8. Card ownership: How should FOOTBALL and trap cards behave if pulled into a hand through Pass, Tackle, Spell Swap or Chaos?
  9. Copycat recursion: Can COPYCAT copy another COPYCAT? Recommended safeguard: no—copy only an earlier, fully resolved Magic effect and never COPYCAT itself.
  10. Chaos / Arcane Heist: Specify whether Magic Cards are protected from being shuffled into the shared deck or stolen as ordinary hand cards.
  11. Phantom Pass: Confirm that the trap card has an indistinguishable back; otherwise its location is visible.
  1. 初始手牌与回合：开局每人分多少功能／得分卡？每回合能出几张？是否回合结束必抽？是否有手牌上限？
  2. 橄榄球卡回收：实体FOOTBALL只有1张，试玩建议得分后放弃牌堆、抽牌堆剩10张或更少时洗回。是否定为正式规则？得分后需不需要补抽？
  3. 反应优先级：Goalpost Warp、Shatter、Spell Swap、Tackle、Interception遇到同一次抽牌时谁先结算？Pass一次抽2张时怎么把FOOTBALL向下移动1张？
  4. “本轮”的持续时间：指当前玩家轮流一圈，还是一直持续到施法者下次回合开始？
  5. Gravity/Gale Force：双倍卡牌是否必须为同名卡？得分卡和一次性魔法卡是否适用？
  6. 额外回合时机：Time Stop与Time Rewind是否在当前回合结束后立刻多进行一回合？
  7. 弃牌、平局：抽牌堆空了怎么办？多人橄榄球数量并列第一怎么办？
  8. 特殊抽牌归属：FOOTBALL、身份陷阱被Pass、Tackle、Spell Swap、Chaos取得时，具体怎样结算？
  9. COPYCAT循环：模仿者能否复制模仿者？建议不能；只能复制此前已经完整结算的其他魔法，避免自我复制无限循环。
  10. CHAOS与ARCANE HEIST：魔法卡是否算普通手牌，是否会被一起洗回或偷走？必须明确。
  11. PHANTOM PASS：身份陷阱卡与普通牌背面是否完全相同？若不同，玩家可看出陷阱位置。
