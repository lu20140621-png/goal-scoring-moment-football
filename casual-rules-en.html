1. Goal & Winning1. 游戏目标与获胜

Collect more mini footballs than anyone else. There are 8 mini footballs in the shared supply.

争夺迷你橄榄球，目标是持有数量最多。中央公共区域共有8个迷你橄榄球。

  1. Regular play ends when all 8 mini footballs have been claimed from the center, or when your 15-minute phone timer expires.
  2. Whichever happens first ends regular play. The player holding the most mini footballs wins.
  1. 公共区的8个迷你橄榄球全部领完，或手机上的15分钟计时结束，常规赛结束。
  2. 哪个条件先满足就按哪个结束，持有迷你橄榄球最多的玩家获胜。

Tie for first: If two or more players are tied for the most mini footballs, only those players enter the Football Scramble tiebreaker.

并列第一：如果两名或以上玩家持有的迷你橄榄球数量并列最多，只有这些玩家进入抢球大战。

2. Game Setup2. 游戏准备

  1. Set aside all Animal Role Cards. They are not used in this mode.
  2. Shuffle the 20 Magic Cards separately. Deal one random Magic Card to each player. Keep the remaining Magic Cards in a separate face-down supply.
  3. Combine all Action Cards and numbered Scoring Cards into one regular deck and shuffle them together. Keep FOOTBALL out for now.
  4. Deal five regular cards to each player.
  5. Only after all starting hands are dealt, add the single FOOTBALL Card to the remaining regular draw pile and shuffle.
  6. Put all eight mini footballs in the center. Choose the starting player and direction. The starting player is Player 1 for tracking rounds.
  7. Use a phone timer and set it for 15 minutes. Start it when setup is complete and the first turn begins.
  1. 把全部动物身份卡放在一旁。本模式不使用身份卡。
  2. 20张魔法卡单独洗牌，每位玩家随机抽取1张。剩余魔法卡单独背面朝下放置。
  3. 把全部功能卡与数字得分卡混在一起洗牌，暂时不要加入FOOTBALL卡。
  4. 每位玩家发5张普通手牌。
  5. 所有玩家都发完牌后，才把唯一的FOOTBALL卡加入剩余的普通抽牌堆，重新洗好。
  6. 把8个迷你橄榄球放在中央，确定首发玩家和行动方向。首发玩家记为1号玩家，用于计算“一轮”。
  7. 使用手机计时器，设置15分钟。准备完成、1号玩家开始行动时启动计时。

↗ The difference between a turn and a round is defined in Chapter 3.

↗ “回合”和“一轮”的区别见第3章。

3. Turns, Rounds & Hand Limit3. 回合、一轮与手牌上限

Turn: one player's individual turn. Round: one complete circuit of play, from the start of Player 1's turn until immediately before Player 1 begins their next turn. Any ongoing Magic Card effect lasts only until the end of that round.
回合（Turn）：单个玩家自己行动的时间。一轮（Round）：从1号玩家开始行动，到1号玩家下一次开始行动之前，算完整一轮。魔法卡产生的持续效果只维持到这一轮结束。
  1. Play cards: On your turn, play any number of eligible Action, numbered Scoring and Magic Cards. Resolve each card and its responses fully before playing another.
  2. Reactions: A card marked REACTION can be played only when its trigger occurs. For reactions to FOOTBALL, follow the call-and-raised-hand order in Chapter 4.
  3. Finish your turn: When you finish playing cards, draw one regular card. If a card explicitly ends your turn, follow that effect instead of taking the normal end-of-turn draw.
  4. Five-card limit: After the normal end-of-turn draw, discard extra regular cards until you hold no more than five. This limit is the same at every player count. Magic Cards do not count; you may temporarily hold more than five regular cards during your turn.
  5. Skipped turn: A PASS skip is attached to the targeted player, not to a seat or direction. The next time that player's turn comes up, they skip exactly that one turn, take no actions and do not draw. Remove the skip after it is used. If STRIP changes direction before the skip is used, the marker stays with that player until their next turn comes up. Once a player's skip has been used, a later direction change does not skip them again. Different players may each have one pending skip, but skips cannot stack on the same player.
  1. 出牌：自己的回合内，可以使用多张符合时机的功能卡、数字得分卡和魔法卡。每张卡必须单独结算完（包括响应），才能使用下一张。
  2. 响应：标注为“响应时使用”的卡牌，只有触发条件出现时才能使用。FOOTBALL相关响应必须遵守第4章的喊牌、举手和先后顺序。
  3. 结束回合：结束主动出牌后，从普通抽牌堆抽1张。若某张卡明确要求立即结束回合，则按卡牌效果执行，不再进行正常的回合结束抽牌。
  4. 5张手牌上限：正常结束抽牌后，自行选择弃牌，直到普通手牌最多剩5张。不管玩家人数多少都一样。魔法卡不计入上限；自己回合中暂时超过5张没有问题。
  5. 跳过回合：PASS产生的跳过标记跟随被指定的玩家，不跟随座位或行动方向。该玩家下一次轮到行动时，必须跳过这一次回合，不能出牌，也不抽牌；执行后立即移除标记。如果标记执行前STRIP改变了方向，标记仍然留在该玩家身上，直到他下一次轮到行动时执行。一次跳过已经执行后，之后方向再次改变，也不会再次跳过同一玩家。不同玩家可以各自拥有一个待执行的跳过标记，但同一玩家不能叠加。

↗ Card abilities and timing are listed once in Chapter 5, Chapter 6 and Chapter 7.

↗ 各卡牌的能力和使用时机分别见第5章、第6章和第7章。

4. FOOTBALL Draws & Reactions4. 抽到FOOTBALL与响应规则

  1. Announce it: There is only one FOOTBALL Card. Whenever you draw it, call out “FOOTBALL!” and pause for reactions. If PASS draws it, the PASS player must announce it too.
  2. Call and raise your hand: Anyone who wants to react must call out the card they are playing and raise a hand during the first response window. Resolve all announced reactions in the order players call out and raise their hands. Close the first response window after every player has had one chance to signal. Once it closes, no new TACKLE may be added to this FOOTBALL event.
  3. TACKLE can steal FOOTBALL: A player who signaled TACKLE in the first response window may take the FOOTBALL Card from whoever currently holds it. Another TACKLE that was already signaled later in the same window may take it back. If FOOTBALL changes hands through TACKLE or INTERCEPTION, the new holder must announce “FOOTBALL is with me!”; this does not reopen the first response window. No player who failed to signal in that window may join later. BLOCK may cancel a TACKLE that targets you.
  4. Resolve the score: After all reactions finish, if the FOOTBALL draw was not stopped by SHATTER or ended by GOALPOST WARP returning the card to the draw pile, the player who holds FOOTBALL at the end takes one mini football from the center. One FOOTBALL draw can award no more than one mini football.
  5. Return FOOTBALL: After the entire draw effect is resolved, return FOOTBALL to the regular draw pile and shuffle it in. If GOALPOST WARP was used, insert it at the chosen top-five position instead; during a multi-card draw, do this only after that full draw finishes.
  6. Multiple-card draws: If an effect draws several cards, complete that full draw and all FOOTBALL reactions before returning FOOTBALL to the pile. If GOALPOST WARP was used, hold FOOTBALL outside the pile until the full draw finishes, then insert it at the chosen top-five position. The single FOOTBALL Card can appear at most once in that one draw effect.
  7. Empty draw pile: If the regular draw pile runs out, shuffle the regular discard pile to make a new draw pile. Used Magic Cards stay in their separate Magic discard pile.
  1. 必须喊出来：实体FOOTBALL卡只有1张。任何玩家抽到后，必须大声喊“FOOTBALL！”，并暂停等待响应。如果是PASS抽到，也必须由出PASS的人喊出来。
  2. 喊牌并举手：想要响应的玩家，必须在第一轮响应窗口内喊出要使用的卡牌名称并举手。按照玩家完成“喊牌＋举手”的先后顺序结算。第一轮响应窗口关闭后，不能再临时加入TACKLE抢球。
  3. TACKLE可以抢橄榄球卡：在第一轮响应窗口内已经举手喊出TACKLE的玩家，可以从当前持有者手中抢走FOOTBALL。后面已经登记、尚未结算的TACKLE，也可以再抢回来。每次持有者改变，都必须喊“FOOTBALL is with me!”（橄榄球卡现在在我这里！）。第一轮没有举手的人，之后不能加入这次FOOTBALL争夺。若TACKLE针对你，可以使用BLOCK取消。
  4. 最终得分：所有响应结算完后，如果没有被SHATTER阻止，也没有因GOALPOST WARP将卡放回抽牌堆而结束事件，最终持有FOOTBALL的玩家从中央领取1个迷你橄榄球。同一次FOOTBALL抽牌事件，最多只能获得1个迷你橄榄球。
  5. FOOTBALL归回牌堆：整次抽牌效果结算完后，把FOOTBALL放回普通抽牌堆并洗好。如果使用了GOALPOST WARP，则按它的能力直接放回牌堆顶部前5个位置之一。
  6. 一次抽多张：如果某个效果要求抽多张卡，必须完成整次抽牌及FOOTBALL响应后，才能把FOOTBALL放回牌堆。如果使用了GOALPOST WARP，先把FOOTBALL放在牌堆外，等整次抽牌结束后，再插入选定的顶部前5个位置。同一次抽牌效果中，FOOTBALL最多出现一次。
  7. 抽牌堆用完：普通抽牌堆没有牌时，把普通弃牌堆洗好，作为新的抽牌堆。已使用的魔法卡仍放在独立的魔法弃牌区。

↗ The exact effects and timing of PASS, TACKLE, INTERCEPTION and BLOCK are in Chapter 5.

↗ PASS、TACKLE、INTERCEPTION和BLOCK的具体能力及使用时机见第5章。

5. Action Cards — All 65. 六种功能卡

Run

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

Draw 1 card.

抽1张卡。

Pass

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

Draw 1 card for yourself and give the next player in the current direction a one-turn skip marker. The marker stays with that player if STRIP changes direction before it is used; see Chapter 3. The same player cannot receive stacked PASS skips, but different players may each have one pending skip. If you draw FOOTBALL, announce it and follow Chapter 4. INTERCEPTION may cancel the skip and take the exact card you drew, even if a TACKLE has already moved it to another player's hand.

自己抽1张卡，并给当前行动方向上的下一位玩家一个跳过标记。如果该标记尚未执行时STRIP改变方向，标记仍然跟随该玩家，具体执行时机见第3章。同一玩家不能叠加多个PASS跳过标记，但不同玩家可以各自拥有一个待执行的标记。如果抽到FOOTBALL，必须喊出来并按照第4章结算。INTERCEPTION可以取消跳过，并拿走你抽到的那张卡；即使这张卡已被TACKLE转移到其他玩家手中，也可以从当前持有者那里拿走。

FOOTBALL REACTIONFOOTBALL响应时使用

You must signal during the first response window. See Chapter 4 for response order and how an already-signaled TACKLE can take it back. BLOCK can cancel a TACKLE that targets you.

自己回合使用时，从任意一名玩家手中抢走1张卡。有人喊出FOOTBALL时，你也可以在第一轮响应窗口内喊“TACKLE！”并举手，从当前持有者手中抢走FOOTBALL。按举手喊牌的先后顺序结算；后面已举手登记的TACKLE可以再抢回来。第一轮响应窗口关闭后，不能再加入TACKLE。若TACKLE针对你，可以使用BLOCK取消。

Interception

REACTION TO PASS响应PASS时使用

Any player may react to another player's PASS, not just the player being skipped. Cancel the skip and take the exact card drawn by the PASS player. If that card is FOOTBALL, announce “FOOTBALL is with me!” and resolve it using Chapter 4. INTERCEPTION takes that exact card even if a TACKLE has already moved it to another player's hand. BLOCK can cancel INTERCEPTION when it targets you.

任何玩家都可以响应别人的PASS，不限被跳过者。取消本次跳过，并拿走PASS玩家刚抽到的那张卡。如果拿到的是FOOTBALL，必须喊“FOOTBALL is with me!（橄榄球卡在我这里！）”，并按照第4章继续结算。即使这张FOOTBALL已经被TACKLE转移到其他玩家手中，INTERCEPTION仍然拿走这张被PASS抽出的卡。若INTERCEPTION针对你，可以使用BLOCK取消。

Block

REACTION响应时使用

When TACKLE or INTERCEPTION targets you, discard BLOCK to cancel that effect.

受到TACKLE或INTERCEPTION影响时，可以弃置BLOCK，取消这次效果。

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

立即反转游戏方向：顺时针改逆时针，逆时针改顺时针。已经产生但尚未执行的PASS跳过标记仍然跟随原目标玩家，直到该玩家下一次轮到行动时执行。实体卡名称固定为STRIP。

7. Magic Cards — All 207. 二十种魔法卡

Each Magic Card is single-use. After use, place it face up in the separate Magic discard pile; Copycat can copy effects only from that pile. Any ongoing Magic effect lasts only until the end of the current round, as defined in Chapter 3. In regular play, exchange 1 mini football for 1 unused Magic Card; return the paid mini football to the shared supply immediately. If no unused Magic Cards remain, you cannot exchange for one. No exchanges are allowed during Football Scramble.

每张魔法卡整局只能使用一次，用后正面朝上放入独立的魔法弃牌区，Copycat只能复制该区域中已经使用过的魔法。所有持续性魔法效果只维持到当前一轮结束（“一轮”的定义见第3章）。常规赛中，使用1个迷你橄榄球兑换1张未使用的魔法卡，支付的球立即放回中央公共区。未使用的魔法卡全部发完后，就不能再兑换。抢球大战期间不能兑换魔法卡。

01 · Time Stop

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

Take one extra turn immediately after your current turn.

本回合结束后，立即额外进行1回合。

02 · Goalpost Warp

REACTION TO FOOTBALL响应FOOTBALL时使用

When another player announces FOOTBALL, call “GOALPOST WARP!” Take FOOTBALL from its current holder and return it face down to any one of the top five positions in the draw pile (counting from the top). This ends the current FOOTBALL event and awards no mini football. Later responses to this card have no target. For multi-card draws, follow Chapter 4's draw-pile timing.

其他玩家宣布抽到FOOTBALL后，喊出“GOALPOST WARP！”。从当前持有者那里拿走FOOTBALL，并将它背面朝下放回抽牌堆顶部前5个位置中的任意一个（从牌顶开始数）。本次FOOTBALL事件立即结束，不获得迷你橄榄球；后续针对这张卡的响应不再有目标。一次抽多张时，按照第4章的抽牌堆结算规则处理。

03 · Flight

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

04 · Gravity

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

For the rest of this round, every other player must use twice the normal number of Action Cards or numbered Scoring Cards to activate those abilities. Magic Cards are not affected. Players protected by Magic Barrier, Invincibility or Guardian are unaffected.

本轮剩余时间内，除自己以外的玩家发动功能卡或数字得分卡能力时，都必须使用正常数量的2倍。魔法卡不受影响。受到Magic Barrier、Invincibility或Guardian保护的玩家不受此效果影响。

05 · Magic Barrier

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

For the rest of this round, other effects cannot cancel your card abilities or steal cards from your hand. Your Action and Scoring Card abilities still require only their normal one card. Gravity and Gale Force do not affect you.

本轮剩余时间内，其他效果不能取消你的卡牌能力，也不能从你手中偷走卡牌。你使用功能卡和得分卡时仍按正常规则出1张即可。重力和狂风魔法对你无效。

06 · Shatter

REACTION TO FOOTBALL响应FOOTBALL时使用

When another player draws FOOTBALL, call “SHATTER!” That FOOTBALL draw awards no mini football. Resolve the remaining card handling using Chapter 4.

其他玩家抽到FOOTBALL时，喊出“SHATTER！”。本次FOOTBALL抽牌不获得迷你橄榄球。之后的卡牌处理按照第4章执行。

07 · Time Rewind

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

Set aside all Action Cards you played this turn. Return them to your hand at the start of your next turn. Their effects stay in play.

将本回合自己打出的全部功能卡收回，单独放在一旁。到自己下一次回合开始时，才将这些卡加入手牌。已经产生的效果全部保留。

08 · Invincibility

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

For the rest of this round, other effects cannot cancel your card abilities or steal cards from your hand. Your Action and Scoring Card abilities still require only their normal one card. Gravity and Gale Force do not affect you.

本轮剩余时间内，其他效果不能取消你的卡牌能力，也不能从你手中偷走卡牌。你使用功能卡和得分卡时仍按正常规则出1张即可。重力和狂风魔法对你无效。

09 · Phantom Pass

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

Insert one unused Animal Role Card into the draw pile as a trap. If a player draws it, take one mini football from that player. If nobody draws it before the game ends, the trap has no effect. All card backs look the same.

拿1张未使用的动物身份卡插入抽牌堆作为陷阱。有人抽到时，从该玩家那里拿走1个迷你橄榄球。如果游戏结束都没人抽到，陷阱失效。所有卡牌背面完全相同，无法从卡背识别。

10 · True Sight

11 · Gale Force

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

For the rest of this round, every other player must use twice the normal number of Action Cards or numbered Scoring Cards to activate those abilities. Magic Cards are not affected. Players protected by Magic Barrier, Invincibility or Guardian are unaffected.

本轮剩余时间内，除自己以外的玩家发动功能卡或数字得分卡能力时，都必须使用正常数量的2倍。魔法卡不受影响。受到Magic Barrier、Invincibility或Guardian保护的玩家不受此效果影响。

12 · Titan's Might

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

For your current turn, double the number of cards drawn by Action Card abilities. Scoring Card draws are not doubled.

本回合功能卡能力产生的抽牌数量×2；得分卡产生的抽牌不翻倍。

13 · Chaos

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

All players shuffle their regular hands into the shared draw pile, then each draws the same number of regular cards they returned. If anyone draws FOOTBALL, announce it and resolve it using Chapter 4. Magic Cards are kept separate.

所有玩家把普通手牌洗回公共抽牌堆，再各自抽取相同数量的普通卡。如果有人抽到FOOTBALL，必须喊出来，并按照第4章结算。魔法卡单独放置，不参与混洗。

14 · Arcane Heist

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

Choose exactly one item to steal: one mini football from any player or the shared center supply; one Magic Card from a player; or one regular card from a player's hand. You may take only one item total, not one of each. You cannot take any item from a player protected by Guardian. Magic Barrier and Invincibility protect cards in their user's hand.

只能选择并偷取一样东西：从任意玩家或中央公共区拿走1个迷你橄榄球；或从一名玩家手中拿走1张魔法卡；或从一名玩家手中拿走1张普通卡。总共只能拿一样，不能每种都拿。不能从受到Guardian保护的玩家那里偷取任何东西。Magic Barrier和Invincibility可以保护使用者手中的卡牌。

15 · Guardian

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

For the rest of this round, other effects cannot cancel your card abilities or steal cards from your hand. Your Action and Scoring Card abilities still require only their normal one card. Gravity and Gale Force do not affect you. Arcane Heist cannot steal any item from you.

本轮剩余时间内，其他效果不能取消你的卡牌能力，也不能从你手中偷走卡牌。你使用功能卡和得分卡时仍按正常规则出1张即可。重力和狂风魔法对你无效。Arcane Heist不能从你这里偷走任何东西。

16 · Copycat

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

Choose one face-up Magic Card from the Magic discard pile. Copy and resolve its effect immediately. You cannot save the copied effect or take that physical card from the discard pile.

只能选择魔法弃牌区内正面朝上的一张已使用魔法卡，复制其技能并立即使用，不能保留复制效果，也不能拿走那张实体卡。

17 · Spell Swap

只能在自己的回合使用，从任意一名拥有迷你橄榄球的对手那里抢走1个迷你橄榄球。

18 · Invisibility

本轮剩余时间内，每当任何玩家开始自己的回合，你都从普通抽牌堆抽1张卡，不管该玩家是否抽牌或使用功能卡。

8. Football Scramble — Sudden Death8. 抢球大战——谁先抢到谁赢

This is a sudden-death race for the single FOOTBALL Card, not another game of collecting eight mini footballs. Follow the setup steps below in order.

抢球大战是争夺唯一一张实体FOOTBALL卡的决胜游戏，不再比较8个迷你橄榄球。请按以下顺序准备。

  1. Who plays: Only the tied leaders participate. Set aside everyone else's cards.
  2. Magic setup: Gather all 20 Magic Cards, including previously used ones. Shuffle them and deal them as evenly as possible among the tied players. Put any remainder aside. Start a fresh, empty face-up Magic discard pile. Magic Cards remain one-use. No buying extra Magic Cards during the scramble.
  3. Mix the regular cards: Gather all Action Cards and all numbered Scoring Cards, mix them together and shuffle. Keep the FOOTBALL Card out for now.
  4. Deal five FIRST: From the complete mixed regular deck, deal exactly 5 cards to each tied player.
  5. THEN randomly remove half of the remaining regular cards: Only after everyone has five cards, randomly remove half of the regular cards still in the draw pile and set them aside. Do not remove cards before dealing or take back anyone's five starting cards.
  6. Insert FOOTBALL FACE UP: Place the one FOOTBALL Card face up somewhere inside the reduced draw pile. Keep it face up while the other cards remain face down. Shuffle the pile well without flipping the FOOTBALL Card face down.
  7. Start: Place the newly prepared draw pile in the center. Begin the 10-minute timer and start the scramble. Regular Action, Scoring and Magic Card effects follow their existing timing rules; there is no Magic-for-mini-football exchange.
  8. FIRST TO GRAB WINS: When the face-up FOOTBALL Card appears during play, players race to physically grab that card. The first player to get the FOOTBALL Card wins the whole match immediately. No eight-mini-football count or additional score comparison.
  1. 确定参赛者：只有常规赛并列第一的玩家参加，其他玩家不参与。
  2. 20张魔法卡：收齐全部20张魔法卡（包括上一局已使用的），重新洗牌，尽可能平均发给参加抢球大战的玩家。不能整除的余牌放一旁，魔法弃牌区重新清空。每张魔法仍只能使用一次，加赛期间禁止用迷你橄榄球换魔法卡。
  3. 功能卡和得分卡混洗：收齐全部功能卡与全部数字得分卡，放在一起彻底洗牌。先不要放FOOTBALL卡。
  4. 先发每人5张：用这副完整的混合普通牌堆，先给每一位加赛玩家发5张牌。
  5. 然后随机移除一半：确保每人已拿到5张后，再从剩余的普通抽牌堆中随机移除一半并放在一旁。不能提前移除，也不能拿走任何玩家已经拿到的5张牌。
  6. FOOTBALL正面朝上插入：将唯一一张FOOTBALL卡正面朝上插到减半后的抽牌堆内部。其他牌仍然背面朝上。保持FOOTBALL正面朝上，把整个抽牌堆重新洗好，不能把FOOTBALL翻成背面。
  7. 正式开局：把准备好的公共抽牌堆放在中央，启动10分钟倒计时，开始抢球大战。普通功能卡、得分卡和魔法卡仍按各自使用时机发动，不允许购买魔法。
  8. 谁先抢到谁赢：在游戏进行中，正面朝上的FOOTBALL卡出现，参赛者立刻争抢这张实体卡；第一个成功抢到FOOTBALL卡的人立即获得整场游戏胜利！不用再凑8个迷你橄榄球，也不用再比较球数。

↗ For the 10-minute timeout and simultaneous-response rules, see Chapter 9.

↗ 10分钟计时结束和同时响应的处理方式见第9章。

9. Special Situations9. 特殊情况处理

Use these rules whenever one of the following situations occurs.

遇到以下情况时，直接按照对应规则处理。

  1. Football Scramble timeout: If the 10-minute phone timer expires while the face-up FOOTBALL Card is available and the contest is still underway, do not stop immediately. Let players finish the current grab and any responses already in progress. Keep the contest open until no player responds. Then end the scramble. The first player to successfully grab the FOOTBALL Card wins.
  2. Magic Card exchange: During regular play, exchange 1 mini football for 1 unused Magic Card and return the paid mini football to the center. If no unused Magic Cards remain, no more exchanges can be made. Magic Card exchanges are not allowed during Football Scramble.
  3. Exactly simultaneous reactions: If two players call out and raise their hands at exactly the same time, both players roll a die. The player with the higher roll chooses which reaction resolves first: their own or the other player's. If the dice show the same number, roll again.
  1. 抢球大战计时结束：如果10分钟手机计时结束时，正面朝上的FOOTBALL卡已经出现，且争抢仍在进行，不要立即停止。让玩家完成当前争抢以及已经开始的响应。继续进行，直到没有玩家再响应，然后结束抢球大战。成功抢到FOOTBALL卡的玩家获胜。
  2. 兑换魔法卡：常规比赛中，使用1个迷你橄榄球兑换1张未使用的魔法卡，并将支付的迷你橄榄球放回公共区。未使用的魔法卡全部发完后，不能再兑换。抢球大战期间不能兑换魔法卡。
  3. 完全同时响应：如果两名玩家同时喊出卡牌名称并举手，双方各掷一次骰子。点数较高的玩家决定哪一方先结算响应：可以选择自己先，也可以选择对方先。如果骰子点数相同，则重新掷骰子。
