1. Goal & Winning1. 游戏目标与获胜

There is only one FOOTBALL Card. When you draw it, announce it aloud and allow your opponents time to react. If the score is not stopped, take one of the 8 mini footballs from the center. After the draw effect is fully resolved, shuffle the FOOTBALL Card back into the draw pile.

实体FOOTBALL卡只有一张。抽到后必须喊出来，给对手响应时间；若无人成功阻止，则从桌面中央共8个迷你橄榄球中拿1个。当前抽牌效果全部结算后，把FOOTBALL卡洗回抽牌堆。

  1. When all 8 mini footballs have been claimed from the center, end regular play and compare each player's footballs.
  2. Or, when the 15-minute timer expires, end regular play and compare footballs.
  3. Whichever occurs first ends regular play. The player holding the most mini footballs wins.
  1. 公共区8个迷你橄榄球全部拿完，立即结束常规赛，比较各玩家持有数量。
  2. 或者15分钟时间到，立即结束常规赛，比较数量。
  3. 以先触发的结束条件为准。持有最多者获胜。
Tied for first: Play a 10-minute Football Scramble. Give each tied player 5 cards before halving the remaining regular draw pile. Insert FOOTBALL face up, reshuffle, then begin. First to physically grab FOOTBALL wins! All 20 Magic Cards are shared evenly; no Magic purchases. See Section 7.
并列第一：进入10分钟抢球大战。先每人发5张 → 剩余抽牌堆减半 → FOOTBALL正面朝上插入 → 洗牌开局。谁先抢到实体FOOTBALL卡谁赢！20张魔法卡尽量平均分配，禁止买魔法。详情见第7节。

2. Setup — Follow This Order2. 开局准备——严格按顺序

  1. Set the Animal Role Cards aside; roles are not used in Mode 3. One unused Role Card may later serve as a Phantom Pass trap.
  2. Shuffle Magic Cards separately. Deal one random Magic Card to each player. Put the unused Magic Cards in their own face-down supply.
  3. Shuffle all Action Cards and numbered Scoring Cards together.
  4. From that mixed deck, deal five cards to each player.
  5. After dealing all five-card hands, insert the single FOOTBALL Card into the remaining regular draw pile and reshuffle.
  6. Place 8 mini footballs in the center, choose the starting player and direction, and start the 15-minute timer as play begins.
  1. 身份卡不用，先放一旁；只有Phantom Pass可能使用其中1张作陷阱。
  2. 魔法卡单独洗牌，随机每人发1张；剩余魔法卡单独放成备用堆。
  3. 把所有功能卡和数字得分卡混合洗牌。
  4. 从混合牌堆每人发5张。
  5. 发完所有人的5张手牌后，才将唯一一张FOOTBALL卡插入剩余公共抽牌堆，再重新洗牌。
  6. 桌面中央摆好8个迷你橄榄球，决定先手与方向，开局启动15分钟倒计时。
Keep these separate: the regular draw/discard piles; unused Magic Cards for exchange; and a face-up Magic discard pile where used spells can be seen and copied.
分开放：普通抽牌堆和弃牌堆；未使用的备用魔法堆；以及使用过的公开魔法弃牌区。

3. Every Turn — Step by Step3. 每个回合——一步一步玩

  1. During your own turn, you may play multiple Action Cards, numbered Scoring Cards and eligible Magic Cards. Check each card’s ON YOUR TURN or REACTION label below. You can play more than one Magic Card if you own more than one.
  2. Resolve one card fully before playing the next. PASS skips only the immediately following player, and repeated PASS skips do not stack. Run, Pass, Tackle, Strip and Spell Swap are own-turn only. Interception is a reaction when another player resolves Pass; Block reacts to Tackle or Interception. Other reaction Magic Cards have their own triggers.
  3. At the end of your turn, draw one card, then discard your choice of excess regular cards until you hold no more than 5. This five-card limit applies to every player count and excludes Magic Cards. A player skipped by PASS has no actions and no normal end-of-turn draw. Effects that explicitly end your turn, such as the 10-point card, override the normal draw, but not the hand limit.
  4. Whenever FOOTBALL is drawn, call it out, give opponents time to react, and only then resolve the mini-football award. After the current draw effect finishes, reshuffle FOOTBALL into the draw pile unless an effect such as Goalpost Warp explicitly places it elsewhere.
  5. If the regular draw pile runs out, shuffle the regular discard pile into a new draw pile. During any multi-card draw, keep already-drawn cards outside the pile until all cards in that draw have been taken. One physical FOOTBALL cannot appear multiple times in the same five-card draw. A 30-point card does not shuffle the deck; only the usual empty-pile recycling rule can require a shuffle while completing its five draws.
  1. 自己回合可打出多张功能卡、多张数字得分卡及多张符合时机的魔法卡；具体请看每张卡下方的“自己回合使用／响应时使用”标记；拥有多张魔法卡时可以使用多张。
  2. 每次只结算一张牌，结算完再出下一张。PASS只让紧接着出牌者的下一位玩家跳过，不能叠加，不影响下下位玩家。Run、Pass、Tackle、Strip、Spell Swap只能自己回合使用。Interception只能在别人Pass时响应；Block用于抵消Tackle或Interception。其他响应魔法按技能时机发动。
  3. 正常回合结束时先抽1张卡，然后把普通手牌弃至最多5张；所有玩家人数都一样。魔法卡不计入5张上限。被PASS跳过者不能行动，也不会进行正常的回合结束抽牌。使用10分卡等明确结束回合的卡时按其效果，不额外正常抽牌，但仍必须整理至最多5张普通手牌。
  4. 抽到FOOTBALL要喊出来并留给对手响应时间，结算后才能领球。当前整个抽牌效果完成后，将FOOTBALL洗回公共牌堆；如果Goalpost Warp等技能已经明确改变其位置，则按该技能处理，不再洗乱。
  5. 抽牌堆没牌时，将普通弃牌堆洗好重新组成抽牌堆。一次要抽多张（比如30分抽5张）时，已抽出来的牌先离开牌堆，等整次抽完才洗回其中的FOOTBALL，同一张FOOTBALL不可能在这5张里出现多次。30分卡本身不会洗牌；仅在公共抽牌堆抽空、必须按通用规则回收弃牌堆时，才会发生额外洗牌。
Hand limit for 2–8 players: At the end of your own turn, after the normal draw, discard any regular cards above five. Choose what to discard. Magic Cards are separate, and players may hold more than five regular cards temporarily during their turn. Skipped turns have no normal end-of-turn draw.
2–8人统一手牌上限：自己回合结束时正常抽1张后，把多出的功能卡／数字得分卡自选弃掉，普通手牌最多5张。魔法卡另放，不计算在内；自己回合中暂时超过5张可以，结束时必须整理。被跳过的回合没有正常结束抽牌。
Buying Magic: Every player starts with one randomly dealt spell. An additional unused Magic Card costs one mini football; immediately return the paid mini football to the shared supply in the center. Spells are one-use only; after use, place them face up in the Magic discard pile. Copycat may copy an effect from that pile, without reclaiming the physical card. No Magic exchange is allowed in the tiebreaker.
魔法换购：每人开局随机有1张。之后用1个迷你橄榄球换1张未使用魔法卡，支付的迷你橄榄球立即放回桌面中央公共区域。魔法卡一张只用一次，用后正面朝上放在魔法弃牌区；Copycat只复制该区域的效果，不能拿回实体卡。抢球大战禁止换购。
Only eight mini footballs exist: A football spent to buy Magic returns to the center and may be earned again. The game ends as soon as the shared supply reaches zero, before another purchase can be made.
始终只有8个迷你橄榄球：买魔法支付的球回到公共区，可以重新被获得；一旦公共区没有球，立即结算，不再继续买魔法。
Scoring Card numbers (30 / 20 / 10 / 6 / 3 / 1) are card labels, not victory points.
得分卡数字30／20／10／6／3／1只用于区分技能，不计入胜负分数。

3A. Follow One Complete Turn3A. 完整回合举例

  1. Start: Everyone has five regular cards and one random Magic Card. FOOTBALL was shuffled into the shared draw deck only after all starting hands were dealt.
  2. Play: On your turn, play any number of eligible cards from your hand, one at a time. Example: play Run, draw one card and finish resolving it; then play Strip to reverse the direction.
  3. PASS + INTERCEPTION: In an A→B→C→D turn order, A plays PASS and A draws 1 card. Only B would skip their next turn; C and D are unaffected. Any other player may react with INTERCEPTION, cancel B's skip and take A's drawn card. Multiple PASS cards do not make B skip more than once; resolve each card separately.
  4. 30-point card: Play it on your turn and immediately draw the top 5 cards, in order. Do not shuffle or rearrange the draw pile. Resolve any FOOTBALL drawn using the separate FOOTBALL rule above.
  5. FOOTBALL: Announce that you drew it. Allow time for Shatter or Goalpost Warp reactions. If no valid effect prevents the score, claim one mini football. Then reshuffle FOOTBALL into the shared deck.
  6. End turn: Draw 1 card, then keep at most 5 regular cards by discarding your choice of extras. Magic Cards are separate and do not count. If skipped by PASS, draw nothing. When the regular deck runs out, recycle its discard pile.
  7. Finish: If the 8 mini footballs have been claimed, or the timer reaches 15 minutes, compare each player's mini footballs. Tied leaders proceed to a 10-minute first-to-grab-FOOTBALL scramble, with a separate setup in Step 7.
  1. 开局：人人有5张普通手牌和1张随机魔法卡；发完手牌之后才把FOOTBALL插入公共抽牌堆洗好。
  2. 出牌：自己的回合可以连续使用多张符合时机的卡，一次结算完一张。例如先出Run抽1张并结算，再出Strip改变方向。
  3. PASS与INTERCEPTION：假设顺序为A→B→C→D，A使用PASS，A自己抽1张，只有紧接着的B跳过下一回合，C、D不受影响。其他任意玩家可打INTERCEPTION，取消B的跳过，并拿走A刚才抽的1张。A多次使用PASS也不能让B连续跳过多回合。每张卡独立结算。
  4. 30分卡：自己的回合使用，立刻从公共抽牌堆顶部按顺序抽5张，不洗牌、不改变牌序。若其中抽到FOOTBALL，单独按照上方FOOTBALL规则结算。
  5. 抽到FOOTBALL：先喊出来，给别人使用Shatter或Goalpost Warp的反应时间；无人成功阻止便领取1个迷你橄榄球。结算结束后洗回FOOTBALL。
  6. 结束回合：先抽1张，然后把普通手牌整理到最多5张（超出的自行选牌弃置）。魔法卡不计入。被PASS跳过不抽牌。普通抽牌堆用完时将普通弃牌重新洗好。
  7. 游戏结束：8个迷你橄榄球领完或15分钟到，比较数量；并列第一者进入10分钟谁先抢到FOOTBALL实体卡谁赢的抢球大战，准备流程见第7节。

4. All 6 Action Cards4. 六种功能卡

Run

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

Draw 1 card.

抽1张卡。

Pass

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

On your turn, draw 1 card. Only the player immediately next in the direction of play skips their next turn. Multiple PASS cards do not stack skips; players after that next player are unaffected.

自己的回合使用，由出PASS的人自己抽1张卡。只有行动顺序紧接着的下一位玩家跳过下一回合，下下位玩家不受影响。多张PASS的跳过效果不叠加。

Tackle

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

Play only on your turn. Take one card from another player's hand.

只能自己回合使用。从另一位玩家手中抽走1张卡。

Interception

REACTION响应时使用

Any player may react to another player's PASS, not just the player being skipped. Cancel the skip and take the 1 card drawn by the PASS player. Resolve this PASS before another card is played.

任何玩家都可以响应别人的PASS（不限被跳过者）。取消该次跳过，并由INTERCEPTION使用者获得PASS玩家抽到的1张卡。结算完这张PASS，再继续其他卡牌。

Block

REACTION响应时使用

When you are targeted by Tackle or Interception, discard Block to cancel that effect.

受到Tackle或Interception影响时，可以弃置Block，取消这次效果。

Strip

ON YOUR TURN自己回合使用

Reverse the direction of play.

立即反转游戏方向：顺时针改逆时针，逆时针改顺时针。实体卡名称固定为Strip。

7. FOOTBALL SCRAMBLE · SUDDEN DEATH (10 MIN)7. 抢球大战 · 谁先抢到谁赢（10分钟）

Only players tied for first enter the scramble. This is a sudden-death race for the one physical FOOTBALL Card, NOT another game of collecting eight mini footballs. Follow the steps in the exact order below.

只有并列第一的玩家参加。抢球大战的目标是谁先抢到唯一一张实体FOOTBALL卡，谁就立即获胜，不再比较8个迷你橄榄球的数量。必须严格按以下顺序准备。

  1. Who plays: Only the tied leaders participate. Set aside everyone else's cards.
  2. Magic setup: Gather all 20 Magic Cards, including previously used ones. Shuffle them and deal them as evenly as possible among the tied players. Put any remainder aside. Start a fresh, empty face-up Magic discard pile. Magic Cards remain one-use. No buying extra Magic Cards during the scramble.
  3. Mix the regular cards: Gather all Action Cards and all numbered Scoring Cards, mix them together and shuffle. Keep the FOOTBALL Card out for now.
  4. Deal five FIRST: From the complete mixed regular deck, deal exactly 5 cards to each tied player.
  5. THEN halve the remaining draw pile: Only after everyone has five cards, randomly remove half of the regular cards still left in the draw pile. Set the removed cards aside. Do not halve the deck before dealing; do not take back anyone's five starting cards.
  6. Insert FOOTBALL FACE UP: Place the one FOOTBALL Card face up somewhere inside the reduced draw pile. Keep it face up while the other cards remain face down. Shuffle the pile well without flipping the FOOTBALL Card face down.
  7. Start: Place the newly prepared draw pile in the center. Begin the 10-minute timer and start the scramble. Regular Action, Scoring and Magic Card effects follow their existing timing rules; there is no Magic-for-mini-football exchange.
  8. FIRST TO GRAB WINS: When the face-up FOOTBALL Card appears during play, players race to physically grab that card. The first player to get the FOOTBALL Card wins the whole match immediately. No eight-mini-football count or additional score comparison.
