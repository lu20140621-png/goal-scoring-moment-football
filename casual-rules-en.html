1. Goal & Winning1. 游戏目标与获胜

There is only one FOOTBALL Card. When you draw it, announce it aloud and allow your opponents time to react. If the score is not stopped, take one of the 8 mini footballs from the center. After the draw effect is fully resolved, shuffle the FOOTBALL Card back into the draw pile.

实体FOOTBALL卡只有一张。抽到后必须喊出来，给对手响应时间；若无人成功阻止，则从桌面中央共8个迷你橄榄球中拿1个。当前抽牌效果全部结算后，把FOOTBALL卡洗回抽牌堆。

  1. When all 8 mini footballs have been claimed from the center, end regular play and compare each player's footballs.
  2. Or, when the 15-minute timer expires, end regular play and compare footballs.
  3. Whichever occurs first ends regular play. The player holding the most mini footballs wins.
  1. 公共区8个迷你橄榄球全部拿完，立即结束常规赛，比较各玩家持有数量。
  2. 或者15分钟时间到，立即结束常规赛，比较数量。
  3. 以先触发的结束条件为准。持有最多者获胜。
Tie for first: Only the tied leaders enter a 10-minute Football Scramble. Follow the normal game rules, but use half the Action Cards, half the numbered Scoring Cards and all 20 Magic Cards, shared as evenly as possible among the tied players. No buying spells during overtime. See Section 7.
并列第一：只有并列第一的玩家进入10分钟抢球大战。玩法与原来相同，功能卡和数字得分卡各取一半，20张魔法卡尽可能平均分发给参赛者，加赛不能换购魔法。详情见第7节。

2. Setup — Follow This Order2. 开局准备——严格按顺序

  1. Set the Animal Role Cards aside; roles are not used in Mode 3. One unused Role Card may later serve as a Phantom Pass trap.
  2. Shuffle Magic Cards separately. Deal one random Magic Card to each player. Put the unused Magic Cards in their own face-down supply.
  3. Shuffle all Action Cards and numbered Scoring Cards together.
  4. From that mixed deck, deal five cards to each player.
  5. After dealing all five-card hands, insert the single FOOTBALL Card into the remaining regular draw pile and reshuffle.
  6. Place 8 mini footballs in the center, choose the starting player and direction, and start the 15-minute timer as play begins.
  1. 身份卡不用，先放一旁；只有Phantom Pass可能使用其中1张作陷阱。
  2. 魔法卡单独洗牌，随机每人发1张；剩余魔法卡单独放成备用堆。
  3. 把所有功能卡和数字得分卡混合洗牌。
  4. 从混合牌堆每人发5张。
  5. 发完所有人的5张手牌后，才将唯一一张FOOTBALL卡插入剩余公共抽牌堆，再重新洗牌。
  6. 桌面中央摆好8个迷你橄榄球，决定先手与方向，开局启动15分钟倒计时。
Keep these separate: the regular draw/discard piles; unused Magic Cards for exchange; and a face-up Magic discard pile where used spells can be seen and copied.
分开放：普通抽牌堆和弃牌堆；未使用的备用魔法堆；以及使用过的公开魔法弃牌区。

3. Every Turn — Step by Step3. 每个回合——一步一步玩

  1. During your own turn, you may play multiple Action Cards, numbered Scoring Cards and eligible Magic Cards. Check each card’s ON YOUR TURN or REACTION label below. You can play more than one Magic Card if you own more than one.
  2. Resolve one card fully before playing the next. Run, Pass, Tackle, Strip and Spell Swap are own-turn only. Interception is a reaction when another player resolves Pass; Block reacts to Tackle or Interception. Other reaction Magic Cards have their own triggers.
  3. At the end of your turn, draw one card. This is the normal end-of-turn draw. A card that explicitly ends your turn (such as the 10-point card) overrides this sequence.
  4. Whenever FOOTBALL is drawn, call it out, give opponents time to react, and only then resolve the mini-football award. After the current draw effect finishes, reshuffle FOOTBALL into the draw pile unless an effect such as Goalpost Warp explicitly places it elsewhere.
  5. If the regular draw pile runs out, shuffle the regular discard pile into a new draw pile. During any multi-card draw, keep already-drawn cards outside the pile until all cards in that draw have been taken. One physical FOOTBALL cannot appear multiple times among the same five simultaneously drawn cards.
  1. 自己回合可打出多张功能卡、多张数字得分卡及多张符合时机的魔法卡；具体请看每张卡下方的“自己回合使用／响应时使用”标记；拥有多张魔法卡时可以使用多张。
  2. 每次只结算一张牌，结算完再出下一张。Run、Pass、Tackle、Strip、Spell Swap只能自己回合使用。Interception只能在别人Pass时响应；Block用于抵消Tackle或Interception。其他响应魔法按技能时机发动。
  3. 正常回合结束时抽1张卡。若打出了明确“立即结束回合”的10分卡，按该卡执行。
  4. 抽到FOOTBALL要喊出来并留给对手响应时间，结算后才能领球。当前整个抽牌效果完成后，将FOOTBALL洗回公共牌堆；如果Goalpost Warp等技能已经明确改变其位置，则按该技能处理，不再洗乱。
  5. 抽牌堆没牌时，将普通弃牌堆洗好重新组成抽牌堆。一次要抽多张（比如30分抽5张）时，已抽出来的牌先离开牌堆，等整次抽完才洗回其中的FOOTBALL，同一张实体FOOTBALL不可能同时出现在这5张里多次。
Buying Magic: Every player starts with one randomly dealt spell. An additional unused Magic Card costs one mini football; immediately return the paid mini football to the shared supply in the center. Spells are one-use only; after use, place them face up in the Magic discard pile. Copycat may copy an effect from that pile, without reclaiming the physical card. No Magic exchange is allowed in the tiebreaker.
魔法换购：每人开局随机有1张。之后用1个迷你橄榄球换1张未使用魔法卡，支付的迷你橄榄球立即放回桌面中央公共区域。魔法卡一张只用一次，用后正面朝上放在魔法弃牌区；Copycat只复制该区域的效果，不能拿回实体卡。抢球大战禁止换购。
Only eight mini footballs exist: A football spent to buy Magic returns to the center and may be earned again. The game ends as soon as the shared supply reaches zero, before another purchase can be made.
始终只有8个迷你橄榄球：买魔法支付的球回到公共区，可以重新被获得；一旦公共区没有球，立即结算，不再继续买魔法。
Scoring Card numbers (30 / 20 / 10 / 6 / 3 / 1) are card labels, not victory points.
得分卡数字30／20／10／6／3／1只用于区分技能，不计入胜负分数。

3A. Follow One Complete Turn3A. 完整回合举例

  1. Start: Everyone has five regular cards and one random Magic Card. FOOTBALL was shuffled into the shared draw deck only after all starting hands were dealt.
  2. Play: On your turn, play any number of eligible cards from your hand, one at a time. Example: play Run, draw one card and finish resolving it; then play Strip to reverse the direction.
  3. Responses: When someone plays Pass, let them draw two, choose their cards and give one away. Interception can respond when the passer is about to keep their chosen card. A valid Block can cancel that Interception. Finish the Pass, then continue.
  4. 30-point card: Draw five physical cards in one draw. Because there is only one FOOTBALL Card, it can appear at most once among these five. Complete the full draw and reaction before reshuffling FOOTBALL.
  5. FOOTBALL: Announce that you drew it. Allow time for Shatter or Goalpost Warp reactions. If no valid effect prevents the score, claim one mini football. Then reshuffle FOOTBALL into the shared deck.
  6. End turn: When you stop playing cards, draw one regular card to end your turn. If the draw pile is empty, reshuffle the regular discard pile to continue. Magic Cards are not recycled into this deck.
  7. Finish: If the 8 mini footballs have been claimed, or the timer reaches 15 minutes, compare each player's mini footballs. Tied leaders proceed to a 10-minute Football Scramble.
  1. 开局：人人有5张普通手牌和1张随机魔法卡；发完手牌之后才把FOOTBALL插入公共抽牌堆洗好。
  2. 出牌：自己的回合可以连续使用多张符合时机的卡，一次结算完一张。例如先出Run抽1张并结算，再出Strip改变方向。
  3. 响应：别人出Pass后，先抽2张、决定留哪张、给谁哪张；他准备留下那张时，其他人可以用Interception响应；Pass玩家可用Block取消Interception。整张Pass结算结束后才能进入下一张卡。
  4. 30分卡：一次抽5张实体卡，由于FOOTBALL只有1张，在这5张中最多出现1次；整次抽牌及响应结束后才将FOOTBALL洗回。
  5. 抽到FOOTBALL：先喊出来，给别人使用Shatter或Goalpost Warp的反应时间；无人成功阻止便领取1个迷你橄榄球。结算结束后洗回FOOTBALL。
  6. 结束回合：主动出牌结束，抽1张卡结束回合。普通抽牌堆空了便将普通弃牌堆重新洗好；魔法弃牌不混入普通牌堆。
  7. 游戏结束：8个迷你橄榄球领完或15分钟到，比较数量；并列第一的玩家进入10分钟抢球大战。

7. Football Scramble — 10-Minute Overtime7. 抢球大战——10分钟加赛

Only players tied for the lead participate. Play with the same card abilities, turns, one-card end-of-turn draw, and FOOTBALL announcement and response rules as the main game, with these changes:

仅限并列第一的玩家参加。卡牌能力、回合流程、结束回合抽一张、FOOTBALL喊出并等待响应等规则全部沿用，只有以下改变：

  1. New scramble: Return all 8 mini footballs to the center, start the tied players at zero, and set a 10-minute timer.
  2. Half the regular cards: Collect the Action Cards and numbered Scoring Cards. Randomly remove half of each category, then shuffle the retained halves together.
  3. Deal: Deal 5 regular cards per tied player where the smaller deck permits. Only after dealing, add the single FOOTBALL Card to the remaining pile and reshuffle.
  4. All 20 Magic Cards: Gather all 20, even those used in the main game. Shuffle and deal them as evenly as possible to the tied players. Set aside any remainder; start an empty Magic discard pile.
  5. No extra Magic purchases: Every Magic Card is still one-use. Used Magic goes face up in the Magic discard pile.
  6. End: The 10-minute timer or the last of 8 mini footballs being claimed ends overtime, whichever happens first. Compare mini-football totals.
  1. 重新开始争球：将8个迷你橄榄球放回桌面中央，仅并列的玩家重新从0球开始，计时改为10分钟。
  2. 普通牌减半：收齐所有功能卡和数字得分卡，分别随机去掉一半，再把保留的两部分合在一起洗牌。
  3. 发牌：减半后牌堆数量足够时，仍按原规则每人发5张普通卡；全部发完以后才把唯一一张FOOTBALL插入剩余牌堆再洗。
  4. 20张魔法平分：收齐整套20张魔法（包括常规赛用过的），重新洗牌后尽可能平均分配给并列玩家；若不能整除，剩余魔法先放一旁。魔法弃牌区清空。
  5. 禁止换购：加赛不能用迷你橄榄球换魔法。每张魔法依然只可使用一次，用完公开放入魔法弃牌区。
  6. 结算：8个球全部领完或10分钟时间到，哪个先发生就比较持球数量。
Still to confirm: If many players are tied, the half-size regular deck may not supply five starting cards each. The rule for another tie after overtime is also not specified.
