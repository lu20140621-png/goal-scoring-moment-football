CASUAL MODE

🏈 Casual Mode

A reversed draw game: everyone wants the FOOTBALL CARD. The first player to collect 3 footballs wins.

Core turn: play optional Action Cards, then make your normal draw to end the turn. Drawing the FOOTBALL CARD gives you 1 football, returns the FOOTBALL CARD to the shared deck, reshuffles it, and makes you draw again.
