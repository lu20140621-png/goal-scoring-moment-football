# TAILS & TACKLES — American Football Game Day Mode

The website's current, authoritative GAME DAY rules are in [game-day-cards.html](game-day-cards.html). Follow the [interactive coach tutorial](strategy-tutorial-en.html) for guided practice. The website entry point is [index.html](index.html).

## Current rules at a glance

- **Minimum matchup:** 2 animal characters versus 2 animal characters. With only **two human players**, each controls two animal characters (one shared Action hand per human). Otherwise, 2v2 / 3v3 / 4v4 teams and mutually agreed larger uneven teams of at least two animals per side.
- **Cards and tabletop:** five card types — Magic, Action, Animal Role, Scoring (18 cards in number-only values 30/20/10/6/3/1), and one Football Card. The red and blue mini-footballs both start at 50 | 50 and track the same field position after a completed Play.
- **Deal:** 7 Action Cards per human per quarter, 2 unique Magic Cards per human for the game, normally 1 animal Role Card per human (2 each in the special two-human match).
- **Plays:** RUN / PASS attack; TACKLE stops RUN, INTERCEPTION steals a PASS, and STRIP is an alternative RUN defense. STRIP rolls one six-sided die: 5–6 forces a fumble and switches possession at the current yard line; 1–4 lets RUN succeed. Only one STRIP attempt per RUN, and the defense cannot add TACKLE afterward. BLOCK counters TACKLE / INTERCEPTION, but not STRIP. Legal Magic/Role responses still apply. Resolve response stacks newest-first; a successful 10-yard gain resets to 1st Down in Game Day.
- **Field position:** move both mini-footballs only after each attack and defense resolves. A successful INTERCEPTION returns both markers to midfield and changes possession.
- **Scoring: 18 cards total.** 30 × 2, 20 × 2, 10 × 3, 6 × 4, 3 × 3 and 1 × 4. Take the card matching each score: TOUCHDOWN = one 6 card, made FIELD GOAL = one 3 card, made EXTRA POINT = one 1 card. The 10/20/30 cards are for exchanging equal-value earned points only (6 + 3 + 1 ↔ 10, 10 + 10 ↔ 20, 20 + 10 ↔ 30). At any time, exchange any combination of cards with the shared supply as long as the total points remain the same; exchanges are restricted by available physical card values, not fixed recipes. For example 6 + 6 + 3 = 15 can become 10 + 3 + 1 + 1, and 6 + 6 + 6 + 3 = 21 can become 20 + 1. Break larger cards into exact values for TIME REWIND. Total each team's printed card values after Quarter 4.
- **Magic and roles:** 20 one-use Magic Cards, 10 animal roles. TIME REWIND reverses the scoring cards earned by a canceled Play; FOX is a once-per-PASS-Play passive; RABBIT and BEAR are unlimited passives. LION and CAT skills are final. EAGLE's SKY PICK is finalized: once each quarter, it strengthens an interception so the same attacking player must play two BLOCK cards together to cancel it.
- **Quarter transitions:** Q1 starts with the opening dice-roll winner; Q2 starts with the other team; Q3 starts with the original winner; Q4 starts with the other team. Every new quarter begins at midfield, 1st Down, with 7 fresh Action Cards per human. Within each quarter, turnovers and scores change possession normally.
- **Team handoff costs a Down:** if the current Football Card holder has no RUN/PASS but an ally can attack, give up ONE Down to hand the Football Card to that teammate before the next Play. Stay at the same field position; advance 1st→2nd→3rd→4th, never reset for free. Giving up 4th Down causes a turnover. In legal field-goal range, the carrier may choose a field goal instead. This is distinct from the mandatory team-wide no-attack procedure.
- **No attacks left:** if nobody on the offense has RUN/PASS, a team in opponent's 40/30/20/10 range must attempt a field goal; otherwise it must surrender possession without a bonus draw. At midfield, no field-goal attempt is allowed. The quarter ends after both teams run out and the active final action resolves.
- **Special rule clarifications:** in the two-human game, name the animal you are using before its action. GRAVITY needs two matching Action Cards from the same player. COPYCAT blindly chooses a random unused opponent/teammate Magic Card, and RACCOON blindly draws a random opponent Action Card.
- **Acrylic Down disks:** The game includes 4 round, double-sided Down disks. One disk shows 1st/2nd on opposite faces, another 3rd/4th. Begin with 1st facing up. 1st→2nd: flip the first disk. 2nd→3rd: switch to the 3rd/4th disk. 3rd→4th: flip the second disk. On a new first down, use the 1st side again. Keep remaining disks nearby. Track Q1–Q4 separately; the red and blue miniature footballs represent yard position only.

The archived [strategy-game-en.html](strategy-game-en.html) is a **legacy solo prototype** and does **not** implement the current tabletop GAME DAY rules.
