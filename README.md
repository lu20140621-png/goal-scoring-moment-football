# TAILS & TACKLES — American Football Game Day Mode

The website's current, authoritative GAME DAY rules are in [game-day-cards.html](game-day-cards.html). Follow the [interactive coach tutorial](strategy-tutorial-en.html) for guided practice. The website entry point is [index.html](index.html).

## Current rules at a glance

- **Minimum matchup:** 2 animal characters versus 2 animal characters. With only **two human players**, each controls two animal characters (one shared Action hand per human). Otherwise, 2v2 / 3v3 / 4v4 teams and mutually agreed larger uneven teams of at least two animals per side.
- **Cards and tabletop:** five card types — Magic, Action, Animal Role, Scoring (bare numbers 6/3/1), and one Football Card. The red and blue mini-footballs both start at 50 | 50 and track the same field position after a completed Play.
- **Deal:** 7 Action Cards per human per quarter, 2 unique Magic Cards per human for the game, normally 1 animal Role Card per human (2 each in the special two-human match).
- **Plays:** RUN / PASS attack; TACKLE / INTERCEPTION / BREAK THROUGH defend; any attacking teammate may respond with BLOCK. Resolve legal response stacks newest-first. A successful 10-yard gain resets the offense to 1st Down.
- **Field position:** move both mini-footballs only after each attack and defense resolves. A successful INTERCEPTION returns both markers to midfield and changes possession.
- **Scoring:** five normal successful 10-yard advances from midfield score a touchdown (6). A successful extra-point kick adds 1; a made two-dice field goal adds 3. Claim matching preprinted Scoring Cards, then total after four quarters.
- **Magic and roles:** 20 one-use Magic Cards, 10 animal roles. TIME REWIND reverses the scoring cards earned by a canceled Play; FOX is a once-per-PASS-Play passive; RABBIT and BEAR are unlimited passives. LION and CAT skills are final. The new EAGLE ability is not yet decided.
- **Quarter transitions:** the ordinary Q1→Q2 / halftime / Q3→Q4 possession and field rule is awaiting a final design decision. Do not treat the NFL procedure as an adopted GAME DAY rule.

The archived [strategy-game-en.html](strategy-game-en.html) is a **legacy solo prototype** and does **not** implement the current tabletop GAME DAY rules.
