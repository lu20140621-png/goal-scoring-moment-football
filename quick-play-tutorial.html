Quick reference: 6 Action Cards
  • RUN: successful rush +10 yards; runner keeps possession
  • PASS: successful pass +10 yards; receiver takes possession
  • TACKLE: blocks a RUN; can be canceled by BLOCK
  • INTERCEPTION: steals an unblocked PASS; reset to 50 | 50
  • BLOCK: cancels TACKLE or INTERCEPTION, not STRIP
  • STRIP: defend RUN only; die 5–6 steals possession at current yard line, 1–4 RUN succeeds unless another legal effect changes the result
Quick reference: 10 animal abilities
  • Dog: PASS as RUN
  • Cat: RUN/PASS as BLOCK
  • Wolf: supply Action Cards for one teammate's play
  • Bear: RUN/PASS as TACKLE
  • Lion: self and one teammate each draw 1 Action Card
  • Tiger: stop ONE opposing Role ability for this quarter (maximum 2 uses per match)
  • Eagle: RUN/PASS as INTERCEPTION
  • Fox: steal 1 random Action Card from each of up to 2 different opposing animal hands
  • Rabbit: one successful RUN advances 20 yards
  • Raccoon: all animal characters draw 1 Action Card

Unless stated otherwise, each animal may use its ability once per quarter.

Quick reference: 20 Magic Cards

Each animal receives two one-use Magic Cards for the entire game. Magic never directly gives you a touchdown. The complete card effects are in Step 6 of the rulebook.
