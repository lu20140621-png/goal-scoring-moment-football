FIRST TO THREE
TOUCHDOWNS WINS.

Follow the same NINE-STEP learning order as Game Day: sort your cards, set up the field, choose teams and kick off, attack, defend, use Magic and animal abilities, learn all six Action Cards, then race to three touchdowns.

QUICK PLAY AT A GLANCE: No 4 Downs. No field goals or extra-point kicks. Each touchdown earns one Scoring Card, regardless of its printed number. Each quarter lasts up to 10 minutes or ends early when neither team can attack. There is no limit on the number of quarters: play until one team scores its third touchdown. Start each new quarter with both markers at 50 | 50 and alternate the starting team.

STEP 1 · PREPARE THE FIVE CARD TYPES

SORT FIRST

Just like Game Day, begin by sorting the cards into five separate types. Do not shuffle different types together.

① Magic Cards · 20 unique cards

Shuffle separately. Deal 2 Magic Cards to each animal character for the entire game, in separate animal hands. Each card can be used only once and is removed from play after use. Unused Magic carries into the next quarter.

② Action Cards · 6 kinds

Run, Pass, Tackle, Interception, Block, Strip. Shuffle these together as one Action Deck. Deal 7 Action Cards to each animal character at the start of each quarter, in separate hands. Used cards enter the Action discard pile.

③ Animal Role Cards · 10 kinds

Each animal character has one hidden Role Card; in a two-human game, each human controls two separate animals. Keep every animal's Role Card and hand separate throughout the match. Quick Play uses its own simpler animal abilities.

④ Scoring Cards · number-only

Keep all Scoring Cards in a shared supply. They are not dealt. After a touchdown, the scoring team takes exactly 1 card. The printed number is ignored—even a card marked 30 counts as one touchdown.

⑤ Football Card · possession

Use the single Football Card to show which animal is carrying the ball. Do not put it inside the Action Deck.

Other pieces you need

Take out the field mat, red and blue mini football position markers, and dice. Do not use the 1st/2nd/3rd/4th Down markers.

STEP 2 · UNFOLD THE FIELD MAT

50 | 50

① Put the mat in the middle

Both teams should be able to see the yard lines and both end zones. The Red side attacks toward the Blue end zone; the Blue side attacks toward the Red end zone.

② Put both mini footballs at midfield

Set the red and blue miniature football markers together at 50 | 50. They show the same current position from each team's perspective. The Football Card, not the mini markers, shows possession.

③ Track every finished play

After a successful 10-yard gain, move both field markers together to the same yard line. Do not move either marker before the attack, defense, and all legal responses finish. If a play fails, leave the markers where they are.

④ Know when to reset to midfield

Reset both markers to 50 | 50 after an interception, after a touchdown, and at the start of each new quarter. An unfinished drive does not carry over to the next quarter.

Field path: An ordinary success advances one position: 50 → opponent 40 → 30 → 20 → 10 → TOUCHDOWN. Some legal animal or Magic effects can advance farther.

STEP 3 · CHOOSE TEAMS, DEAL & KICK OFF

START PLAYING

① Choose teams

Red vs. Blue, with at least two animal characters on each team. Standard play: 4 humans (2v2), 6 humans (3v3) or 8 humans (4v4). With two humans, give each person two animal roles for a 2v2 character game; each animal has its own separate hand of 7 Action Cards per quarter and 2 Magic Cards per game. One human manages two independent animal hands; never combine them.

② Deal animal identities

Shuffle and deal roles face down. Each human privately checks each animal Role Card they control, keeping the identities hidden until the corresponding abilities activate. An animal revealed once stays visible for the rest of the match.

③ Deal two Magic Cards

Deal 2 random Magic Cards to each animal character, once at the beginning. Keep each animal's Magic hand separate; no new Magic Cards are dealt in later quarters. After use, remove the card from the game.

④ Deal Action Cards

Shuffle the six types of Action Cards together. Deal 7 Action Cards to each animal character for Quarter 1, keeping the hands separate. The remaining deck is available only when a rule says to draw. Do not automatically draw after a play.

⑤ Roll dice for the first kickoff

Each team chooses one representative. Agree whether the higher or lower roll wins, then each rolls one die; re-roll ties. The winner receives the Football Card for Quarter 1 and names the first ball carrier.

⑥ Start the quarter timer

Set a 10-minute timer. Remember which team started Quarter 1: the other team starts Quarter 2, then alternate every new quarter (Red → Blue → Red → Blue, or vice versa).

STEP 4 · HOW TO ATTACK

NO 4 DOWNS
50→40→30→20→10→TOUCHDOWN
  1. Only the ball carrier initiates an ordinary attack. Play Run or Pass; a teammate may supply a needed card only through a legal team-assist rule such as Wolf's ability.
  2. Run: Successful run = 10 yards forward. The same animal keeps the Football Card.
  3. Pass: Name the receiving teammate before defenders respond. Successful pass = 10 yards forward; give the Football Card to that receiver.
  4. Allow the other team to defend. Resolve all legal Action responses, animal abilities and Magic effects before moving both mini field markers.
  5. Stopped attack: Gain zero yards and keep the current possession unless Interception steals it. There are no four attempts or down counters. A team with another legal attack can keep going.
  6. Out of attack cards: If your team has no playable Run/Pass (including an available animal conversion or Wolf's assistance) and the opponent can still attack, give the Football Card to the opponent at the current field position. If neither team can attack, end the quarter immediately and follow Step 9.
  7. Touchdown: A successful play that reaches the opposing end zone scores one touchdown. Take one Scoring Card as a marker. Reset both markers to midfield; the team that was scored on takes the Football Card unless this was the third touchdown, which ends the match.

Fast rule: Ordinary Run/Pass advances 10 yards, Rabbit's activated successful Run advances 20. Quick Play has no FIELD GOAL, EXTRA POINT or PUNT.

STEP 5 · HOW TO DEFEND

REACT & COUNTER

① Respond to Run

Play Tackle to stop a Run, or Strip to risk stealing the ball. Strip requires one six-sided die: 5–6 forces a fumble and gives the defense the Football Card at the current yard line; 1–4 lets the Run succeed normally. Only one Strip attempt per Run; after trying Strip, the defense may not play Tackle or another ordinary defense Action Card on that Run. Legal Magic/Role effects may still apply.

② Respond to Pass

Play Interception to steal possession and reset both markers to 50 | 50. Strip cannot defend Pass.

③ Counter the first defense

Any attacking teammate may play Block to cancel a normal Tackle or Interception. Block does not cancel Strip. Once Strip is chosen on a Run, no defending teammate may follow it with another ordinary defensive Action Card on that same Run. Apply Wolf and Cat conversions when activated.

④ Resolve responses, then move

Players may respond with legal Action Cards, Magic or Role abilities at the correct time. Resolve the newest response first, then complete the original attack. Keep the markers unchanged until the final outcome.

If the entire defending team makes no defensive response to an ordinary successful Run or Pass, that team may have one defending animal draw one Action Card into its own hand from the undealt deck (if any remain). A stopped attack does not earn this draw.

The six Action Cards work the same way as in Game Day. Their full quick-reference table is in Step 8. What changes in Quick Play is the no-downs system, not the Action Card effects.

STEP 6 · ALL 20 MAGIC CARDS

QUICK PLAY V2

2 per animal for the whole match

Shuffle the 20 Magic Cards separately, then deal 2 to each animal character, keeping hands separate. No Magic Cards are redealt when the quarter changes.

One use, then out

After a Magic Card is played, take it out of the game, even if countered. Unused Magic stays in your hand. Magic affects game actions, but never directly awards a touchdown or a Scoring Card.

🪄 01 · Time Stop

On your team's Run or Pass, choose one opposing animal character. That animal cannot play Action Cards until this Play ends; other defending animals, even one controlled by the same human, may respond.

One use per match

🪄 02 · Goalpost Warp

Before an opposing Run/Pass, move both markers 10 yards back toward that attacker’s own side. Can only be used when the attacker has already advanced past 50 | 50.

One use per match

🪄 03 · Flight

Before defenses are played against your Run, make that Run immune to Tackle and Strip. Magic may still counter.

One use per match

🪄 04 · Gravity

After an opponent plays Run/Pass, they must supply one additional matching Run/Pass card or their attack fails. Wolf may provide it using Team Assist.

One use per match

🪄 05 · Magic Barrier

Cancel one opposing Run or Pass before it resolves. Neither field position nor possession changes. Shatter can cancel this barrier.

One use per match

🪄 06 · Shatter

Play as a response to cancel one opposing Magic Card before it resolves. Cannot cancel an Action Card or animal skill.

One use per match

🪄 07 · Time Rewind

Immediately before an opponent’s successful play is finalized, cancel it and restore its starting ball carrier and field position. Used cards remain spent. Never undo a touchdown after its Scoring Card is taken.

One use per match

🪄 08 · Invincibility

Before opponents defend your Run/Pass, prevent Tackle, Interception and Strip from defending that attack. Magic may still counter.

One use per match

🪄 09 · Phantom Pass

Play Pass and name a receiver, then hide a mini football in your left or right hand. One defender guesses. Right guess: Pass fails. Wrong guess: Pass succeeds for 20 yards. The guessing game replaces ordinary Action defense; True Sight and Shatter can counter.

One use per match

🪄 10 · True Sight

Choose one: secretly view one opposing animal's separate Action hand, OR counter Phantom Pass by finding the hidden ball so that Pass fails.

One use per match

🪄 11 · Gale Force

On an opponent’s successful Pass, reduce the distance advanced by 10 yards, to a minimum of zero. If successful, the receiver still gets the Football Card.

One use per match

🪄 12 · Titan's Might

Play with your Run. If that Run succeeds, advance 20 yards instead of 10. Strip and Tackle may still defend.

One use per match

🪄 13 · Chaos

Before your Run/Pass, bench one allied animal (not the carrier) until this quarter ends. That animal cannot act or use its Role ability or Magic. This attack cannot be defended with ordinary Action Cards; if successful, it advances 20 yards. Magic still applies.

One use per match

🪄 14 · Arcane Heist

Randomly take up to 2 Action Cards from ONE opposing animal character's hand. If that human controls two animals, choose only one animal for this spell. Add the stolen cards to the casting animal's hand. Never steal Magic or Role Cards.

One use per match

🪄 15 · Guardian

When an opposing Magic or animal ability would steal/discard your team’s Action Cards or disable one of your animals, cancel that effect. Does not block normal defensive Action Cards.

One use per match

🪄 16 · Copycat

Copy the effect of one previously used Magic Card from the out-of-game pile (not Copycat). Obey its original timing and targets. The original Magic remains used.

One use per match

🪄 17 · Spell Swap

Trade one of the casting animal's unused Magic Cards for one unused Magic Card held by another animal, teammate or opponent. Keep the cards in the receiving animals' separate Magic hands. Neither card activates during the trade.

One use per match

🪄 18 · Invisibility

Play before your Pass. Opponents may not defend it with Interception. If successful, it still advances 10 yards and the receiver gains possession. Magic may counter.

One use per match

🪄 19 · Roll of Fate

Play with a Run or Pass and roll one die instead of normal Action defense. Roll 1–3: attack fails. Roll 4–6: it succeeds for 20 yards. Resolve the original Run/Pass carrier or receiver normally. Magic can counter.

One use per match

🪄 20 · Mystic Fog

Each animal counts its own Action Cards. Shuffle all animals' held Action Cards into the shared Action Deck; each animal then draws back the same number into its own separate hand. Magic and Role Cards are unaffected.

One use per match

These are Quick Play-specific V2 playtest effects. Game Day’s Magic effects remain separate. No kicks, extra points, or four-down rules are used here. A spell must be played at its stated timing; all other legally timed Magic responses still apply.

STEP 7 · ALL 10 ANIMAL ROLE CARDS

QUICK PLAY · CARD TEXT

Every animal may use its ability once per quarter, except Tiger: at most twice per match and once per quarter. All skills and card hands belong to individual animal characters, not human players. Each animal keeps its own Action Cards, Magic Cards and Role Card, even when one human controls two animals. “You” means the animal using the skill; “teammate” means a different allied animal. Reveal the acting animal's Role Card when its ability first activates and keep it face up. Cards may transfer between animal hands only when a specific effect allows it.

🐶 Dog

When Dog attacks, Dog may play 1 Pass card as a Run card. Resolve it using the normal Run rules.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🐱 Cat

When a Block is legal, Cat may play 1 Run or Pass card as a Block card from Cat's own hand.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🐺 Wolf

During one Play, Wolf may supply Action Cards from Wolf's own hand for one allied animal's Run, Pass or legal Block response, including multiple required Block cards. For that Play, those cards count as the chosen allied animal's cards.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🐻 Bear

When defending an opposing animal's Run, Bear may play 1 Run or Pass card from Bear's own hand as a Tackle card.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🦁 Lion

Lion and 1 chosen allied animal each draw 1 Action Card from the shared Action Deck into their own separate hands.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🐯 Tiger

Tiger chooses 1 opposing animal. That animal cannot use its Role ability for the rest of this quarter. Its Action Cards and Magic Cards are unaffected.

MAX 2 PER MATCH · MAX 1 PER QUARTER

🦅 Eagle

When defending an opposing animal's Pass, Eagle may play 1 Run or Pass card from Eagle's own hand as an Interception card.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🦊 Fox

Randomly steal 1 Action Card from each of up to 2 different opposing animals' separate Action hands. If one human controls both target animals, they still count as two different targets. Add the stolen cards to Fox's own Action hand.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🐰 Rabbit

Declare this ability when Rabbit plays a Run card. If Rabbit's Run succeeds, advance 20 yards instead of 10.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🦝 Raccoon

Every animal character on both teams draws 1 Action Card from the shared Action Deck into its own separate hand.

ONCE PER QUARTER

Timing and limits: Declare an ability before it resolves. A used ability is spent even if its effect is countered or its attack fails. At a new quarter, all animal skills refresh except Tiger's match-wide limit. Converted RUN/PASS cards follow the rules of the Action type they become. Wolf assists teammate animals only, within one Play, using cards from Wolf's separate hand; it does not give the Football Card to Wolf. All Role-use limits and card transfers apply to each animal independently, even when one human controls two animals. Fox targets up to two different opposing animal characters, not two different humans. Each targeted animal must have an Action Card. In a two-human 2v2 match, Fox may take one random Action Card from each of the two opposing animals, even though one human controls both. Never take two from the same animal through Fox's skill. Arcane Heist instead targets ONE opposing animal hand for up to two cards. Lion and Raccoon draw per eligible animal from the remaining Action Deck only while cards are available. Tiger disables only one opposing Role ability, never its Action or Magic Cards, and cannot undo an effect already resolved.

STEP 8 · ALL SIX ACTION CARDS

6 CARD TYPES · Strip

Use these six and only six Action Card types in Quick Play. There are no fourth-down or field-goal Action Cards.

RunCarrier plays Run; success advances 10 yards. Same animal keeps possession. Defenders may Tackle or attempt Strip.
PassCarrier announces a teammate receiver; success advances 10 yards and transfers possession to that receiver. Interception may stop it.
TackleDefend against Run, cancel its gain, and keep the Football Card with the attacker. Block may cancel Tackle.

Strip: roll 1–4

The fumble attempt fails and Run succeeds normally. The defense cannot follow up with Tackle.

Strip: roll 5–6

Card-conversion rules: Dog can treat Pass as Run; Cat can treat Run/Pass as Block; Bear can treat Run/Pass as Tackle; Eagle can treat Run/Pass as Interception. Each requires their once-per-quarter animal ability. Ordinary Block cannot block Strip.

STEP 9 · SCORE TOUCHDOWNS & WIN

FIRST TO 3

HOW TO COUNT TOUCHDOWNS

  1. Successfully advance the Football Card into the opposing end zone.
  2. The scoring team takes one Scoring Card from the shared supply. It counts as one touchdown, no matter what number is printed on it.
  3. If that team now has 3 Scoring Cards, it wins immediately.
  4. Otherwise, return both field markers to 50 | 50. The team that conceded the touchdown takes the Football Card and begins the next possession. The quarter timer keeps running unless the quarter has ended.
THAT'S IT. No point totals, no field goals, no extra points, no four-down countdown, and no fixed number of quarters. The first team with THREE TOUCHDOWNS wins.

WHEN THE QUARTER ENDS

End condition A: time is up

Set a 10-minute timer for each quarter. When time runs out, finish resolving the play already in progress, then end the quarter.

End condition B: both teams are out of attacks

End the quarter early if neither team has any legal way to start another Run or Pass, including any currently available animal-card conversion or teammate assistance. A defense-only hand does not count as an attack.

End of quarter: The current yard line does not matter—even if the markers show 40, 30, 20 or 10, that position is cleared. Gather and reshuffle the Action Cards, redeal 7 Action Cards to each animal character in separate hands, and set BOTH field markers back to 50 | 50. All Animal Role abilities refresh for the new quarter, except a role with a whole-game limit. Keep the same animal identities and all still-unused Magic Cards; never redeal spent Magic.

Next kickoff: The teams alternate who starts each quarter, regardless of who ended the last quarter with possession. Example: Q1 Red starts → Q2 Blue starts → Q3 Red starts → Q4 Blue starts → Q5 Red starts, and so on. The designated starting team chooses its ball carrier.

Important: There is no four-quarter cap and no points-based tie. The only match end is a team's third touchdown.

Quick example: Red starts Quarter 1 and finishes with the ball at Blue's 10-yard line. The 10-minute timer expires—or neither team can attack. End the quarter right there; the 10-yard position does not carry over. Both field markers return to 50 | 50, action hands are redealt, animal abilities refresh (subject to Tiger's match limit), and Blue receives the Football Card to start Quarter 2. Keep all touchdown counters and any unused Magic Cards.
