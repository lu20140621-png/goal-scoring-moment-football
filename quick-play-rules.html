FIRST TO THREE
TOUCHDOWNS WINS.

Follow the same NINE-STEP learning order as Game Day: sort your cards, set up the field, choose teams and kick off, attack, defend, use Magic and animal abilities, learn all six Action Cards, then race to three touchdowns.

QUICK PLAY AT A GLANCE: No 4 Downs. No field goals or extra-point kicks. Each touchdown earns one Scoring Card, regardless of its printed number. Each quarter lasts up to 10 minutes or ends early when neither team can attack. There is no limit on the number of quarters: play until one team scores its third touchdown. Start each new quarter with both markers at 50 | 50 and alternate the starting team.

STEP 1 · PREPARE THE FIVE CARD TYPES

SORT FIRST

Just like Game Day, begin by sorting the cards into five separate types. Do not shuffle different types together.

① Magic Cards · 20 Unique Cards

Shuffle separately. Deal 2 Magic Cards to each animal for the whole match. Each animal keeps a separate Magic hand. In a two-human game, each person controls two animals and manages two separate 2-card Magic hands. Each card can be used only once and is removed from play after use. Unused Magic carries into the next quarter.

② Action Cards · 6 Kinds

Run, Pass, Tackle, Interception, Block, Strip. Shuffle these together as one Action Deck. Deal 7 Action Cards to each animal character at the start of each quarter, in separate hands. Used cards enter the Action discard pile.

③ Role Cards · 10 Kinds

Each animal character has one hidden Role Card; in a two-human game, each human controls two separate animals. Keep every animal's Role Card and hand separate throughout the match. Quick Play uses its own simpler animal abilities.

④ Scoring Cards · Number-only

Keep all Scoring Cards in a shared supply. They are not dealt. After a touchdown, the scoring team takes exactly 1 card. The printed number is ignored—even a card marked 30 counts as one touchdown.

⑤ FOOTBALL Card · Possession

Use the single FOOTBALL Card to show which animal is carrying the ball. Do not put it inside the Action Deck.

Other Pieces You Need

Take out the field mat, red and blue mini football position markers, and dice. Do not use the 1st/2nd/3rd/4th Down markers.

STEP 2 · UNFOLD THE FIELD MAT

50 | 50

① Put the Mat in the Middle

Both teams should be able to see the yard lines and both end zones. The Red side attacks toward the Blue end zone; the Blue side attacks toward the Red end zone.

② Put Both Mini Footballs at Midfield

Set the red and blue miniature football markers together at 50 | 50. They show the same current position from each team's perspective. The FOOTBALL Card, not the mini markers, shows possession.

③ Track Every Finished Play

After a successful 10-yard gain, move both field markers together to the same yard line. Do not move either marker before the attack, defense, and all legal responses finish. If a play fails, leave the markers where they are.

④ Know When to Reset to Midfield

Reset both markers to 50 | 50 after an interception, after a touchdown, and at the start of each new quarter. An unfinished drive does not carry over to the next quarter.

Field path: An ordinary success advances one position: 50 → opponent 40 → 30 → 20 → 10 → TOUCHDOWN. Some legal animal or Magic effects can advance farther.

STEP 3 · CHOOSE TEAMS, DEAL & KICK OFF

START PLAYING

① Choose Teams

Red vs. Blue, with a minimum of two animal characters per team. Standard matchups: 2v2, 3v3 or 4v4 animals. In a two-human game, each person controls two animals, with separate 7-Action-Card and 2-card Magic hands for each animal.

② Deal Animal Identities

Shuffle and deal roles face down. Each human privately checks each Role Card they control, keeping the identities hidden until the corresponding abilities activate. An animal revealed once stays visible for the rest of the match.

③ Deal Two Magic Cards

Deal 2 Magic Cards to each animal once for the entire match, keeping each animal's Magic hand separate. In a two-human match, each human manages two separate 2-card Magic hands and must name the casting animal. Magic Cards are never redealt in later Quarters; used Magic is removed from the game.

④ Deal Action Cards

Shuffle the six types of Action Cards together. Deal 7 Action Cards to each animal character for Quarter 1, keeping the hands separate. The remaining deck is available only when a rule says to draw. Do not automatically draw after a play.

⑤ Roll Dice for the First Kickoff

Each team chooses one representative. Agree whether the higher or lower roll wins, then each rolls one die; re-roll ties. The winner receives the FOOTBALL Card for Quarter 1 and names the first ball carrier.

⑥ Start the Quarter Timer

Set a 10-minute timer. The starting team enters the Preparation Phase, names the carrier, and announces “We're ready! [Animal] has the ball!” before playing RUN/PASS. Remember which team started Quarter 1: the other team starts Quarter 2, then alternate every new quarter (Red → Blue → Red → Blue, or vice versa).

STEP 4 · HOW TO ATTACK

PREPARE → PLAY → RESOLVE
50→40→30→20→10→TOUCHDOWN

THREE SIMPLE TERMS: Preparation Phase means time before the next attack. A Play (one attack round) is exactly one RUN or PASS plus all legal responses until the result is final. A Quarter contains many Plays and lasts up to 10 minutes, or ends early when neither team can attack (Step 9).

1 · Preparation: Name the Carrier

Before a RUN/PASS is played, the attacking team may hand off the FOOTBALL Card freely between its active teammates, without using any card or gaining yards. The team must then announce “We're ready! [Animal] has the ball!” so the defense knows exactly who holds the ball. Resolve any legal Preparation-timing Magic or Role abilities now.

2 · Play: Run or Pass

Only the announced carrier starts the Play. RUN succeeds for 10 yards and the same animal keeps possession. PASS names an eligible allied receiver before responses; on success, advance 10 yards and transfer the FOOTBALL Card to that receiver. Wolf may assist a teammate at its specified trigger.

3 · Defend and Resolve

The opposing team may respond with a legal defense, and players can use legal Role or Magic reactions. No free handoff is allowed after RUN/PASS is declared, while the Play is being resolved. Only a successful PASS, INTERCEPTION, STRIP or another explicitly stated effect can transfer the FOOTBALL Card. Move both miniature field markers only after responses finish.

4 · Start a New Preparation

If TACKLE stops RUN or a Play fails without possession changing, the same team keeps the FOOTBALL Card at the current position and enters a new Preparation Phase. If a defender steals possession with INTERCEPTION or STRIP, the defender's team becomes the offense, starts its own Preparation Phase, and declares its new carrier before attacking.

Example: “Red is ready! Dog has the ball!” Dog plays RUN → Blue plays TACKLE → Red plays BLOCK. The TACKLE is canceled and, unless a legal Magic/Role changes the outcome, RUN advances 10 yards. Blue cannot play a second TACKLE against that same RUN. Before the next attack, enter a new Preparation Phase and announce “We're ready!” again.

When the entire offense cannot attack: First check whether an active teammate can use RUN/PASS, a legal card conversion (such as Dog), Wolf assistance, or a legal once-per-Quarter draw ability. If none can attack but the opposing team can, give that team the FOOTBALL Card at the same physical position, facing the opposite end zone. Example: Red loses the ball at Blue's 10-yard line; Blue starts from its own 10, not 10 yards from scoring. If neither team has a legal attack after any allowed draw effects, end the Quarter (Step 9).

Touchdown: If the carrier successfully advances into the opposing end zone, give opponents the immediate response window (including TIME REWIND) before awarding exactly one Scoring Card. Reset both markers to 50 | 50; unless this was the winning third touchdown, the team that conceded possession enters a new Preparation Phase.

No four-down countdown, field goals, extra-point kicks or punts. An ordinary successful RUN/PASS advances 10 yards; specific Role or Magic effects may change the distance.

STEP 5 · HOW TO DEFEND

ONE NORMAL DEFENSE PER PLAY

Run → Tackle → Block

The defense may play one TACKLE against RUN. Any eligible attacking teammate may answer with one BLOCK, which cancels that TACKLE. No second TACKLE can be played against the same RUN. Legal Magic/Role responses still apply.

Run → Strip → Dice

Instead of TACKLE, the defense may play one STRIP. After legal Magic/Role reactions, roll one die: 5–6 forces a fumble and transfers the FOOTBALL Card at the existing physical yard line; 1–4 means STRIP fails and RUN succeeds unless another legal effect prevents it. BLOCK cannot cancel STRIP; no TACKLE may follow STRIP during the same Play.

Pass → Interception → Block

The defense may play one INTERCEPTION against PASS. If uncanceled, possession changes and both field markers reset to 50 | 50. An attacking teammate may play one BLOCK to cancel INTERCEPTION. No second INTERCEPTION can be played against the same PASS. STRIP cannot defend PASS.

Role and Magic Responses

Normal defenses are limited to one per Play. There is no limit on the number of different Magic Cards played during a Play, as long as each card has a legal trigger. Each Magic Card is single-use for the entire match.

If the entire defending team plays no defensive Action Card, Role skill or Magic Card against an ordinary successful RUN/PASS, one defender may draw one Action Card from the undealt Action Deck, if any remain. This is one optional draw for the entire team, not for every animal.

After the Play: An attack stopped without a steal means the same offense prepares again. INTERCEPTION or successful STRIP makes the defense the new offense, which must declare its carrier and “We're ready!” before playing RUN/PASS.

STEP 6 · ALL 20 MAGIC CARDS

QUICK PLAY V2

2 Magic Cards per Animal

At the beginning of the match, deal exactly 2 Magic Cards to each animal. Keep all animal Magic hands separate. In a two-human 2v2 match, each human manages two separate 2-card Magic hands (4 cards total). Name the animal casting a spell.

One Use, Then Out of the Game

Every Magic Card can be played once per match. After it is used, put it in the used-Magic pile, even if countered. The card is not restored after a Play or between Quarters. Exception — SPELL SWAP: During Preparation, trade it for one unused Magic Card from another animal's Magic hand. You may use the Magic Card you receive at its normal timing. The new holder of SPELL SWAP may use it starting next Quarter; after it is used, put it in the used-Magic pile. If no unused Magic Card is available in another animal's Magic hand, SPELL SWAP cannot be played. Unlimited legal Magic Cards may be played during one Play; using many early means having fewer later.

Two Timing Classes

FREE-CHOICE / PREPARATION: May be used when you choose during the Preparation Phase, before RUN/PASS starts. TRIGGERED / REACTION: May be used only when the named Action Card, Magic Card or result occurs. Attack-boosting Magic must be declared with the attack, before it resolves. COPYCAT adopts the trigger of the Magic being copied.

Duration & Stacking

Protection, offense boosts and restrictions created for a Play last only for that single Play. They expire when the Play resolves. CHAOS is the explicit exception: the selected ally cannot use Action Cards or its Role ability for the rest of that Quarter, but can still use its unused Magic at legal triggers. Already-resolved changes to hand ownership and field position remain. There is no cap on how many Magic Cards can be stacked per Play. Two effects both saying “advance 20 instead of 10” do not add up to 40; they each replace the normal distance.

WHEN TO USE EACH MAGIC: PREPARATION: GOALPOST WARP, ARCANE HEIST, SPELL SWAP, MYSTIC FOG, and TRUE SIGHT (inspect); CHAOS immediately before your attack. WHEN YOUR RUN/PASS BEGINS: TIME STOP, FLIGHT, INVINCIBILITY, PHANTOM PASS, TITAN'S MIGHT, INVISIBILITY and ROLL OF FATE. REACTIONS: GRAVITY, MAGIC BARRIER, SHATTER, TIME REWIND, GALE FORCE, GUARDIAN and TRUE SIGHT (counter PHANTOM PASS). COPYCAT follows the original Magic's timing. Magic never directly awards Scoring Cards or spends previously earned scores.

🪄 01 · Time Stop

When your team declares RUN or PASS, choose one opposing animal. Only that animal cannot play Action Cards for the rest of this Play. Other animals and legal Magic Cards may still respond.

One use per match

🪄 02 · Goalpost Warp

Just before an opponent starts RUN or PASS, move both field markers 10 yards toward that attacker's own end zone. Use this only if the attacker has already advanced beyond midfield.

One use per match

🪄 03 · Flight

When you play RUN, opponents cannot use TACKLE or STRIP against that RUN only. Magic may still counter it.

One use per match

🪄 04 · Gravity

When an opponent plays RUN or PASS, the attacker must provide one additional matching Action Card or the attack fails. Wolf may supply it through Team Assist.

One use per match

🪄 05 · Magic Barrier

Cancel one opposing RUN or PASS before it resolves. Field position and possession stay unchanged. SHATTER may cancel this barrier.

One use per match

🪄 06 · Shatter

Play in response to an opposing Magic Card to cancel that card's effect. Cannot cancel Action Cards or animal Role abilities.

One use per match

🪄 07 · Time Rewind

Just before an opponent's successful play is finalized, cancel it and restore the starting field position and ball carrier. Played cards remain spent. You cannot undo a touchdown after its Scoring Card has been claimed.

One use per match

🪄 08 · Invincibility

Before opponents defend your RUN or PASS, prevent TACKLE, INTERCEPTION and STRIP from defending this Play only. Magic may still respond.

One use per match

🪄 09 · Phantom Pass

Play PASS and name the receiver. Hide a mini football in your left or right hand and let one defender guess. A correct guess makes the PASS fail; a wrong guess makes it succeed for 20 yards. This guessing contest replaces ordinary Action Card defense. TRUE SIGHT or SHATTER may counter.

One use per match

🪄 10 · True Sight

Choose one: secretly inspect one opposing animal's Action Card hand, or counter PHANTOM PASS by revealing which hand hides the ball, making that PASS fail.

One use per match

🪄 11 · Gale Force

When an opponent's PASS succeeds, reduce its advance by 10 yards (minimum zero). The receiver still takes the FOOTBALL Card.

One use per match

🪄 12 · Titan's Might

Play this with your RUN. If that RUN succeeds, advance 20 yards instead of 10. TACKLE and STRIP may still defend.

One use per match

🪄 13 · Chaos

Immediately before your RUN or PASS, choose one active allied animal (not the carrier). Until this Quarter ends, that ally cannot play Action Cards or activate its Role ability, and cannot receive PASS. The ally's unused Magic Cards remain usable at their normal triggers. Only your current attack ignores ordinary defensive Action Cards and advances 20 yards if successful. Magic may counter CHAOS. If SHATTER cancels CHAOS before it resolves, the ally is not restricted, but CHAOS is still spent. In 2v2, CHAOS cannot boost PASS if the only possible receiver was restricted.

One use per match

🪄 14 · Arcane Heist

Choose one opposing animal and randomly take up to two Action Cards from its own hand. Add them to the casting animal's hand. Never take Magic or Role Cards.

One use per match

🪄 15 · Guardian

When an opposing Magic Card or Role ability would steal or discard your team's Action Cards or disable one allied Role ability, cancel that effect. GUARDIAN does not cancel ordinary defensive Action Cards.

One use per match

🪄 16 · Copycat

Copy the effect of one previously used Magic Card from the out-of-game pile, except COPYCAT. You must obey that Magic Card's original timing and target requirements. The original card remains used.

One use per match

🪄 17 · Spell Swap

During Preparation, trade this card for one unused Magic Card from another animal's Magic hand. You may use the Magic Card you receive at its normal timing. The new holder of SPELL SWAP may use it starting next Quarter. If no unused Magic Card is available in another animal's Magic hand, SPELL SWAP cannot be played.

New holder may use next Quarter

🪄 18 · Invisibility

Play this before your PASS. Opponents cannot use INTERCEPTION against this PASS only. If it succeeds, advance 10 yards and give the FOOTBALL Card to the receiver. Magic may still counter.

One use per match

🪄 19 · Roll of Fate

Play this with RUN or PASS. Roll one six-sided die instead of allowing ordinary Action Card defense: 1–3 means the attack fails; 4–6 means it succeeds and advances 20 yards. Keep or transfer possession according to the original RUN or PASS. Magic may still counter.

One use per match

🪄 20 · Mystic Fog

Each animal counts the Action Cards in its own hand. Shuffle all players' held Action Cards into the shared Action Deck, then let every animal draw back the same number into its separate hand. Magic and Role Cards are unaffected.

One use per match

These are Quick Play-specific effects. Game Day’s Magic effects remain separate. No kicks, extra points, or four-down rules are used here. A spell must be played at its stated timing; all other legally timed Magic responses still apply.

STEP 7 · ALL 10 ROLE CARDS

QUICK PLAY · CARD TEXT

Every animal may use its ability once per quarter, except Tiger: at most twice per match and once per quarter. Role abilities and Action hands belong to individual animal characters. Each animal keeps its own Action hand and Role Card. Magic Cards also belong to individual animals (2 per animal for the entire game). In a two-human game, each person manages two separate animal Action hands and two separate 2-card Magic hands. “You” in an animal skill means that animal; “teammate” means a different allied animal. Reveal the acting animal's Role Card when its ability first activates and keep it face up. Cards may transfer between animal hands only when a specific effect allows it.

🐶 Dog

When attacking, you may play 1 PASS card as RUN. Resolve it using the normal RUN rules.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🦊 Fox

Choose up to two different opposing animals. Randomly take 1 Action Card from each chosen animal's separate Action hand and add them to Fox's Action hand. Both animals may be controlled by the same human. Never take two cards from the same animal.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🐰 Rabbit

Declare this ability when you play RUN. If your RUN succeeds, advance 20 yards instead of 10.

ONCE PER QUARTER

🦝 Raccoon

ONCE PER QUARTER

Timing and limits: Declare an ability before it resolves. A used ability is spent even if its effect is countered or its attack fails. At a new quarter, all animal skills refresh except Tiger's match-wide limit. Converted RUN/PASS cards follow the rules of the Action type they become. Wolf assists teammate animals only, within one Play, using cards from Wolf's separate hand; it does not give the FOOTBALL Card to Wolf. All Role-use limits, Action Card transfers, and Magic hands apply separately to each animal, even when one human controls two animals. Fox targets up to two different opposing animals. Each must have an eligible Action Card. In a two-human 2v2 match, Fox may take one card from each of the two opposing animals, even though one human controls both. Arcane Heist instead targets ONE opposing animal hand for up to two cards. Lion draws for itself and one allied animal; Raccoon makes each animal draw once into its own Action hand, including when one human controls two animals. Draws use the shared undealt Action Deck only while cards are available. Tiger disables only one opposing Role ability, never its Action or Magic Cards, and cannot undo an effect already resolved.

STEP 8 · ALL SIX ACTION CARDS

6 CARD TYPES · Strip

Use these six and only six Action Card types in Quick Play. There are no fourth-down or field-goal Action Cards.

Action CardQuick Play effect
RunCarrier plays Run; success advances 10 yards. Same animal keeps possession. Defenders may Tackle or attempt Strip.
PassCarrier announces a teammate receiver; success advances 10 yards and transfers possession to that receiver. Interception may stop it.
TackleDefend against Run, cancel its gain, and keep the FOOTBALL Card with the attacker. BLOCK may cancel this TACKLE; the defense cannot play a second TACKLE against the same RUN.
InterceptionDefend against Pass. If it stands, steal the FOOTBALL Card and reset BOTH field markers to 50 | 50. BLOCK may cancel this INTERCEPTION; the defense cannot play a second INTERCEPTION against the same PASS.
BlockAny attacking teammate may cancel one Tackle or Interception. Block cannot cancel Strip.
StripDefend Run only instead of Tackle. Roll one die after legal reactions: 5–6 force a fumble and take possession at the current yard line; 1–4 Run succeeds normally. Only one STRIP per RUN; no TACKLE or other ordinary defense Action may follow STRIP in the same Play. Block cannot cancel Strip.

Strip: Roll 1–4

The fumble attempt fails and RUN succeeds unless another legal Magic/Role effect stops it. The defense cannot follow up with Tackle.

Strip: Roll 5–6

Force a fumble. The Strip defender takes the FOOTBALL Card at the existing yard line. Do not reset the markers for a fumble.

Card-conversion rules: Dog can treat Pass as Run; Cat can treat Run/Pass as Block; Bear can treat Run/Pass as Tackle; Eagle can treat Run/Pass as Interception. Each requires their once-per-quarter animal ability. Ordinary Block cannot block Strip.

STEP 9 · SCORE TOUCHDOWNS & WIN

FIRST TO 3

How to Count Touchdowns

  1. Successfully advance the FOOTBALL Card into the opposing end zone.
  2. The scoring team takes one Scoring Card from the shared supply. It counts as one touchdown, no matter what number is printed on it.
  3. Complete any legal immediate response, including TIME REWIND, before taking the Scoring Card. Once claimed, a Scoring Card confirms that touchdown and it cannot be undone later.
  4. If that team now has 3 Scoring Cards, it wins immediately.
  5. Otherwise, return both field markers to 50 | 50. The team that conceded the touchdown takes the FOOTBALL Card and begins the next possession. The quarter timer keeps running unless the quarter has ended.
THAT'S IT. No point totals, no field goals, no extra points, no four-down countdown, and no fixed number of quarters. The first team with THREE TOUCHDOWNS wins.

When the Quarter Ends

End Condition a: Time Is Up

Set a 10-minute timer for each quarter. When time runs out, finish resolving the play already in progress, then end the quarter.

End Condition B: Both Teams Are Out of Attacks

End the Quarter early when neither team can start a RUN or PASS with its remaining Action Cards, animal conversions or Wolf's help. Before declaring the Quarter over, a player may choose to use an eligible unused card-draw ability, such as LION or RACCOON (once per animal per Quarter), or spend a one-use Magic such as MYSTIC FOG. After drawing, check again: if an attack is now possible, return to Preparation; otherwise finish the Quarter if no further legal draw will be used. A draw ability that was already used this Quarter cannot be repeated.

End of quarter: The current yard line does not matter—even if the markers show 40, 30, 20 or 10, that position is cleared. Gather all Action Cards from hands, the undealt pile and the Action discard pile; shuffle and redeal 7 Action Cards to each animal, keeping animal hands separate. Set BOTH field markers back to 50 | 50. Reset Role abilities for the new Quarter except any match-wide limit. Keep all identities and each animal's unused Magic Cards in its separate hand; used Magic is never redealt. CHAOS restrictions expire at the Quarter boundary.

Next kickoff: The teams alternate who starts each quarter, regardless of who ended the last quarter with possession. Example: Q1 Red starts → Q2 Blue starts → Q3 Red starts → Q4 Blue starts → Q5 Red starts, and so on. The designated starting team enters Preparation, chooses its carrier and announces “We're ready! [Animal] has the ball!” before the next Play.

Important: There is no four-quarter cap and no points-based tie. The only match end is a team's third touchdown.

Quick example: Red starts Quarter 1 and finishes with the ball at Blue's 10-yard line. The 10-minute timer expires—or neither team can attack. End the quarter right there; the 10-yard position does not carry over. Both field markers return to 50 | 50, action hands are redealt, animal abilities refresh (subject to Tiger's match limit), and Blue receives the FOOTBALL Card to start Quarter 2. Keep all touchdown counters and any unused Magic Cards.
