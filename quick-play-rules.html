1. GET READY TO PLAY

SETUP

Choose your teams

Play Red Team vs. Blue Team. Use at least two animal characters on each side (2v2, 3v3, or 4v4). With only two human players, each controls two animal characters but uses one shared hand.

Deal the cards

Each human gets 1 hidden Animal Role Card (2 roles each in a two-human game), 2 random Magic Cards for the entire match, and 7 Action Cards at the start of each quarter. Put unused cards in their separate supplies. Reveal an Animal Role when its ability is first used; keep it face up thereafter.

Set the mat and pieces

Place the red and blue mini football field markers together at 50 | 50. The markers show the same field position from opposite team directions. Use the single Football Card to show which animal possesses the ball. Keep dice ready for kickoff and applicable card effects.

Make a touchdown counter

Set the numbered Scoring Cards next to the mat. In Quick Play, each completed touchdown earns exactly ONE Scoring Card of any printed value. Ignore all printed numbers. These cards are only markers; do not deal them into the Action or Magic decks.

First kickoff: Each team chooses one person to roll one die. Before rolling, agree whether the higher or lower result wins. Re-roll ties. The winning side begins Quarter 1 with the Football Card at midfield. The opposite team will start Quarter 2, and the opening team will start Quarter 3, alternating for as many quarters as needed.

2. ATTACK & ADVANCE

NO DOWNS
50→40→30→20→10→TOUCHDOWN
  1. The animal holding the Football Card starts a play with RUN or PASS. A teammate may assist only when a rule or animal ability allows it.
  2. RUN: If it succeeds, advance 10 yards. The runner keeps possession.
  3. PASS: Choose an allied receiver before the defense responds. If it succeeds, advance 10 yards and give the Football Card to that receiver.
  4. Give defenders a chance to respond. Resolve all legal counters, Magic Cards, and animal skills before moving both mini football markers together to the resulting field position.
  5. When a play is stopped, stay at the same yard line. There are no 1st–4th Downs, no lost downs, no punt, and no field-goal or extra-point kicks. The team holding possession may attack again if it still has a playable attack.
  6. When the current carrier has no playable attack but an ally does, the Football Card may be handed to that teammate before the next play; this does not advance the field.

Fast rule: Ordinary success = 10 yards. Rabbit's activated RUN = 20 yards. If a legal advance reaches the opponent's end zone, score a touchdown. No separate kick or die roll is needed to score.

3. DEFEND & COUNTER

SAME ACTION CARDS
Action CardWhat it does in Quick Play
RUNStart a rushing attack; success advances 10 yards and the runner keeps the Football Card.
PASSStart a passing attack to a named teammate; success advances 10 yards and the receiver gets the Football Card.
TACKLEDefend against RUN; if it stands, the run fails at its starting yard line.
INTERCEPTIONDefend against PASS; if it stands, the interceptor's team takes possession and both markers reset to 50 | 50.
BLOCKAttackers may cancel a TACKLE or INTERCEPTION with a legal BLOCK response. Any attacking teammate may help by playing BLOCK.
BREAK THROUGHDefenders may stop RUN or PASS directly, or respond after a defense has been canceled. The play fails at its original yard line.

Take turns making legal responses to a play. Resolve the last response first, then the earlier responses. If the whole defending team chooses not to respond to an ordinary successful RUN/PASS, one defending player may draw one Action Card from the undealt deck (if any remain), using the same rule as Game Day.

4. WHEN DOES A QUARTER END?

10 MIN · UNLIMITED QUARTERS

End condition A: time is up

Set a 10-minute timer for each quarter. When time runs out, finish resolving the play already in progress, then end the quarter.

End condition B: both teams are out of attacks

End the quarter early if neither team has any legal way to start another RUN or PASS, including any currently available animal-card conversion or teammate assistance. A defense-only hand does not count as an attack.

End of quarter: The current yard line does not matter—even if the markers show 40, 30, 20 or 10, that position is cleared. Gather and reshuffle the Action Cards, redeal 7 to each human, and set BOTH field markers back to 50 | 50. All Animal Role abilities refresh for the new quarter, except a role with a whole-game limit. Keep the same animal identities and all still-unused Magic Cards; never redeal spent Magic.

Next kickoff: The teams alternate who starts each quarter, regardless of who ended the last quarter with possession. Example: Q1 Red starts → Q2 Blue starts → Q3 Red starts → Q4 Blue starts → Q5 Red starts, and so on. The designated starting team chooses its ball carrier.

Important: There is no four-quarter cap and no points-based tie. The only match end is a team's third touchdown.

5. ALL 10 ANIMAL ABILITIES

QUICK PLAY EFFECTS

Each animal may use its ability once per quarter, except Tiger: once per quarter, twice per match total. Announce the ability, reveal the Role Card if still hidden, and turn or mark that card sideways until the next quarter. Played Action Cards go to the Action discard pile.

🐶 DOG · Flexible Play

Play one PASS as a RUN instead. Resolve it as RUN: on success, gain 10 yards and keep possession.

Once per quarter

🐱 CAT · Quick Block

Use one RUN or PASS as a BLOCK when a legal BLOCK response is needed.

Once per quarter

🐺 WOLF · Team Assist

Help a teammate play an Action Card from your own hand, including supplying a RUN/PASS for that teammate's attack or helping complete a requirement for multiple BLOCK cards. This one assist may supply all cards needed for that one action, if you have them. The teammate remains the acting player or ball carrier.

Once per quarter · teammates only

🐻 BEAR · Heavy Tackle

Play one RUN or PASS as TACKLE to defend an opponent's RUN.

Once per quarter

🦁 LION · Team Draw

Draw one Action Card for yourself and one for a chosen teammate from the undealt Action deck, if cards remain.

Once per quarter

🐯 TIGER · Intimidate

Choose one opposing animal. That animal cannot activate its Role ability for the rest of this quarter. Magic Cards and Action Cards are not affected. Already-resolved abilities are not undone.

Max 2 times per match · max 1 per quarter

🦅 EAGLE · Sky Pick

Play one RUN or PASS as INTERCEPTION to defend an opponent's PASS.

Once per quarter

🦊 FOX · Double Steal

Randomly take one Action Card from each of two different opposing players. Never take two from the same human player. If fewer than two different opponents have cards, take only the available eligible cards.

Once per quarter

🐰 RABBIT · Sprint

Activate on your own RUN. If it succeeds, advance 20 yards instead of 10.

Once per quarter

🦝 RACCOON · Everyone Draws

Every human player, on both teams, draws one Action Card from the undealt deck, if available.

Once per quarter

In a two-human game, two animal roles on one side share that human's hand. Fox cannot take two cards from the same human, even if that human controls two different animals. Animal skills refresh between quarters unless their match-wide limit has been reached; Tiger never gets a third use.

6. MAGIC CARDS

2 PER PERSON · ONE USE EACH

Deal only once

Shuffle the 20 Magic Cards and deal exactly two to each human player at the beginning of the match. Keep the cards you haven't used when a new quarter starts.

Play it, then discard it

A Magic Card is a one-time effect. After using it, place it outside the game. Never refresh Magic Cards at quarter changes. Magic does not use up an animal ability.

Quick Play magic effects are still being tuned. The 20 Game Day Magic Cards use the same physical deck, but effects that refer to field-goal kicks, extra points, four downs, or printed score values do not carry over unchanged to this mode. Their Quick Play wording will be added here after it is finalized; do not treat Game Day's kicking rules as part of Quick Play.

7. SCORE 3 TOUCHDOWNS TO WIN!

WIN CONDITION
  1. Successfully advance the Football Card into the opposing end zone.
  2. The scoring team takes one Scoring Card from the shared supply. It counts as one touchdown, no matter what number is printed on it.
  3. If that team now has 3 Scoring Cards, it wins immediately.
  4. Otherwise, return both field markers to 50 | 50. The team that conceded the touchdown takes the Football Card and begins the next possession. The quarter timer keeps running unless the quarter has ended.
THAT'S IT. No point totals, no field goals, no extra points, no four-down countdown, and no fixed number of quarters. The first team with THREE TOUCHDOWNS wins.
